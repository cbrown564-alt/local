# Owner-operated impossible site

Deferred combination brief. Written 9 August 2026.

## Status

Do not build this yet.

Build and review the pure owner’s operating page first. Then build and review
the pure impossible local website. This brief becomes active only when both
have produced direct evidence about what should survive.

The full specifications are:

- [`owners-operating-page-spec.md`](owners-operating-page-spec.md)
- [`impossible-local-website-spec.md`](impossible-local-website-spec.md)

## Proposition

The combined form is an extraordinary public website whose changing creative
state is controlled by one small, truthful owner operation.

It is not an impossible site with a CMS attached. It is not an operating page
wearing a spectacular skin. The owner’s real act must change the public
experience in a way visitors can feel and use.

Examples of the relationship, not settled concepts:

- a farm publishes the weekly harvest and the public landscape visibly changes
  to show what the week is made of;
- a workshop changes its current focus and the public journey resolves toward
  the work it can honestly take;
- a venue sets tonight’s state and the entire page moves from preparation to
  arrival to closed, while preserving a useful next action.

The right subject will be chosen after the first two prototypes. Do not assume
it is their synthetic food business or mountain-bike workshop.

## The one invariant

> One simple owner decision must produce one meaningful public transformation.

If the owner has to art-direct the experience, it fails. If the visitor cannot
tell what changed or act differently because of it, it fails.

## What the first two prototypes must teach us

From the owner’s operating page:

- the smallest operation an owner can sustain;
- the language owners understand without training;
- the safe draft, publish and rollback boundary;
- which public states remain useful when closed or sparse;
- whether direct editing helps or distracts.

From the impossible local website:

- which singular visual idea remains meaningful after repeated use;
- how much motion and rendering complexity the experience can justify;
- the strongest reduced-motion and renderer-failure forms;
- whether the final business action feels native to the creative journey;
- which parts are subject-specific and must never become a framework.

## Entry gate

Write the full specification only when all of these are true:

1. The owner loop has been completed on a phone, including failed publish and
   rollback.
2. The operating page has convincing open, closed, sparse and full states.
3. The impossible site has a complete five-chapter journey and useful action.
4. Its high-ground moment works in full motion and reduced motion.
5. We can name one owner-controlled field that should change the impossible
   experience without requiring creative judgement.
6. We can explain the combination without using “dynamic,” “immersive,”
   “personalised” or “living” as a substitute for the actual behaviour.

## First representative slice

When the gate opens, build one state change only:

1. the owner changes a real operating state;
2. previews its public consequence;
3. publishes safely;
4. a visitor receives a substantially different but still legible experience;
5. the visitor completes the relevant action;
6. rollback restores the prior public experience.

No second content type, theme system or general creative-state engine until
that loop works.

## Creative standard

The combination should be bolder than either parent because the operational
truth gives the visual transformation a reason to exist. It must still retain
the simplicity of one owner act and one visitor consequence.

It should not reuse the current portfolio’s shells, layouts or house effects.
It should not converge into a generic “real-time website” product. The result
must be authored for one business and maintainable by that business.

## Promotion boundary

Even after implementation, this remains a representative prototype until a
participating business owns the accounts, names the person responsible for the
changing state, records a baseline action and completes a dated review. The
combination is the most ambitious form and therefore needs the clearest
distinction between implemented, verified, validated and promoted.
