# Elevation brief priorities — the remaining concepts, ranked

Written 4 August 2026. The method these briefs are derived by is
`docs/the-elevation-method.md`; the hotel instance is
`docs/good-to-great-concept-elevation.md`. This document ranks the concepts
that do not yet have an elevation brief, in the order they would most benefit
from one, so the next brief is chosen deliberately rather than by whichever
concept happens to be open.

Update the status line of a concept's entry here when its brief is written;
do not re-derive this ranking from scratch each time.

## Scope

**Briefed (five):** Scopers, Betty's Better Butters, Donard Veterinary,
Mourne Cycles, Dundrum Inn — briefs in `research/concepts/<slug>/`, all
implemented.

**Elevated without a derived brief (one):** Hotel Enniskeen — the flagship;
six of the seven good-to-great moves shipped 31 July 2026.

**Ranked here (twelve):** every other public concept.

**Out of scope:** Murdock Brothers (held — trading unconfirmed; no brief
until the unblock condition in `PROSPECTS.md` is met); Tonn Ruray and Groves
Chemist (retired, not candidates for republication); any assessed business
without a published concept.

## How the ranking reads

A concept benefits from an elevation brief in proportion to four things:

1. **Unmined magic** — voice, geography, ritual, arc and mark sitting in
   `research/pipeline/verifications.json` that the page does not use.
2. **The gap** — how far the current page is from recognition and theatre
   today (a single functional screen has further to rise than a built-out
   site).
3. **Live pitch value** — the concept is still a working argument to a
   business that has not answered, so a better page changes a real outcome.
4. **Feasibility from the record** — the moves can be made honestly from
   what is already verified, without needing owner-supplied material first.

A rich record with a blocked lever (imagery rights, a replied-to pitch)
ranks below a moderate record the brief can use immediately.

### 1. Hugh McCann's — the wedding venue with the fullest record and the thinnest page

**Brief written 4 August 2026:**
`research/concepts/hugh-mccanns/hugh-mccanns-elevation-brief.md`.
**First pass (moves 1–4) implemented 4 August 2026;** moves 5 (the dusk
visualisation) and 6 (the where-your-day-happens plate) wait on their asset
sessions.

`research/pipeline/verifications.json` calls it the highest-value single
target in its batch and sets the accuracy bar to match. The record holds the
method's favourite raw materials in every head: their voice ("From Today
Until Your Day, We Do"; "traditional rustic charm as well as the new world
contemporary style"), their arc (three generations in a two-hundred-year-old
building), their geography (Central Promenade, the gardens, the Mournes
through the dining-room windows), their ritual (the booked date — the whole
business runs on it). The current concept is a competent feature proposal:
one faithful dining-room visualisation, a date-and-guest-count mailto, and
placeholder navigation. Nothing stages the day the venue actually sells.
The essence is close to a paradox the method asks for: a venue whose entire
trade is future dates has no way to ask about one — and the concept already
names that gap, so the brief builds on a honest foundation rather than
repairing one.

### 2. Castle Farm Fresh Produce — the least-elevated public page

**Brief written 4 August 2026:**
`research/concepts/castle-farm/castle-farm-elevation-brief.md`.

The concept is a single hero screen: placeholder navigation, an inert
basket, a steps strip and a proof band. Every other public concept has had
more passes. The shape is Product/Counter, and the record holds a real
ritual — the weekly box: choose a box, add the staples, collect or get it
delivered across Northern Ireland, with a published order-minimum policy and
a live £45 medium mixed box as a price anchor. That weekly rhythm is exactly
the material the Betty's and Scopers briefs were built from, and the
publication record already points at the second surface (a weekly box
builder or category shelf). The voice head is thin — a Shopify store, no
harvested sentence of their own — so, per the method, the thin inventory is
itself a finding: the brief's discipline is to make much of the box, the
delivery map and the family-farm claim rather than to add to them.

### 3. Newcastle Family Dental Care — a typographic page in a proven register

**Brief written 4 August 2026:**
`research/concepts/newcastle-dental/newcastle-dental-elevation-brief.md`.

The publication record says it plainly: the page is entirely typographic
today. The Care shape's elevation register is already worked out — the
Donard Veterinary brief proved that reassurance, not spectacle, is the
theatre — but the method's warning applies: the brief must be derived from
this practice's record, not copied from the vet's. What the record holds:
three named dentists with their degrees, the Iveagh Court address, the
Mon–Fri hours, and an essence no competitor shares — a practice with its own
name, trading under someone else's roof, reached over plain HTTP. The
concept's padlock address bar is its one recognition move and currently
carries the whole page. Caveat to keep the brief honest: the commercial
relationship to the DJ Maguire group is unestablished, so the brief should
assume nothing about who decides.

### 4. Kent Amusements — fifty summers on the promenade

**Brief written 4 August 2026:**
`research/concepts/kent-amusements/kent-amusements-elevation-brief.md`.

The strongest keepable-artifact candidate among the twelve: over fifty years
on the same Central Promenade site, with a BBC *Your Place and Mine* entry
in the sources — an arc and a geography that want a drawn plate the way
Enniskeen's twelve acres did. The business is also the most naturally
theatrical left unbriefed: dodgems, arcade machines, new-for-2026 VR — the
theatre register is joy, and the current page is a quiet single screen with
an empty hours layout the publication record already flags. The swap test
fails today: any seaside arcade could wear this page. The constraint the
brief must respect: no confirmed seasonal hours exist anywhere, so the
"today" moves must stay honest handoffs rather than computed boards.

### 5. Cúpla — the name is the essence

**Brief written 4 August 2026:**
`research/concepts/cupla/cupla-elevation-brief.md`.

The method says the essence is usually a paradox; Cúpla hands it over
wrapped: the Irish for twins, opened by twin owners, every label twinned in
Irish and English. The concept already does real recognition work — the
twin-ring mark, the paired bilingual copy — so the gap is smaller than the
four above. What is missing is theatre: the counter ritual (morning bakes
into brunch bowls — a day-part structure the register supports), the
owners' story told as the pair, and one moment worth showing to the other
twin. The brief is shorter and lower-risk than most here, with one named
dependency carried from the publication record: a fluent Irish speaker must
review the bilingual menu before client use.

### 6. Kelly, McEvoy & Brown — a fifty-three-year arc, handled gently

**Brief written 4 August 2026:**
`research/concepts/kelly-mcevoy-brown/kelly-mcevoy-brown-elevation-brief.md`.

Established 1973 with "Build On Experience" as its own words and thirteen
named projects across six sectors — churches, hospitals, schools and
community halls from Magheralin to Crossmaglen. The keepable artifact
writes itself: a drawn map of built work across the counties, the
contractor's equivalent of the estate plate. Two things hold it at six. It
was republished on 3 August 2026 after two repair cycles (an identity
blocker, then a false case-study premise), so the next pass should be a
brief first and a quiet build second — no third destabilising surprise. And
the imagery lever is blocked: two of thirteen projects carry a photograph,
the rest wait on confirmed rights, which caps the theatre a brief can
honestly promise.

### 7. The Tool Centre — a staged mechanism waiting on its voice

**Brief written 4 August 2026:**
`research/concepts/tool-centre/tool-centre-elevation-brief.md`.

The concept already stages the business's real mechanism — the hire desk
with its "day rate on request" board — and the shelf-versus-yard tension
(hardware retail in front, plant hire out the back, two names on Facebook)
is genuinely its own. What the record has not yet given the page is voice:
Google reviews praising the owner and the prices are noted in verification
but never harvested, and no sentence of the shop's own is on record. The
best-sentence test is the brief's first job here. Below the top five
because the recognition delta is editorial rather than structural, and the
Trade register's big moves (the range mapped, the ride) belong to the
already-briefed Mourne Cycles — this brief must find the counter's own
version, not borrow the bike shop's.

### 8. Newcastle Chamber of Commerce — the map business, already built out

**Brief written 4 August 2026:**
`research/concepts/newcastle-chamber/newcastle-chamber-elevation-brief.md`.

The Chamber is the town's map business, and a drawn Main Street plate of
member trades would be the natural keepable artifact — for the Chamber and
for every business on it. It sits at eight because the concept is already
the most built-out of the twelve: six linked pages, a working directory
search, category rails, events and a join route, with the prototypes
history behind it. The elevation delta is real but incremental. The
constraints are named in the record: the member list is illustrative and
unconfirmed, the committee is volunteer-run and reformed in 2023, so the
brief's moves must not depend on a confirmed membership the Chamber has not
supplied.

### 9. Painted Earth — rich material, blocked lever

**Brief written 4 August 2026:**
`research/concepts/painted-earth/painted-earth-elevation-brief.md`.

Ninety-two named makers, an on-site goldsmith, an upstairs gallery opened
April 2025, twenty-plus years trading, and their own sentence already on the
page ("A small team with a big dream"). By unmined magic alone this ranks
far higher. It sits at nine because the theatre lever is blocked: the
concept cannot show the work — every piece is a labelled drawn placeholder —
and the recorded first improvement is to swap in the shop's own photography
the moment it engages. A brief written now would mostly queue moves behind
that dependency. It was also published on 3 August 2026 on the project
owner's decision against a reviewer recommendation; the honest next artefact
for this concept is the engagement conversation, not another studio pass.
The brief, when written, should mine the maker register and the
collection-only ritual — recognition that does not need photography.

### 10. Douglas & Cromie — recognition already shipped

The concept's central move *is* the recognition: the dealer's real archived
wordmark, palette, typography, vehicle photography and "drive away with
confidence" wording, restored from the Internet Archive after the domain
stopped resolving. What a brief could add is honest but modest: the
fifty-year arc told as a band, the aftercare promise staged as the ritual,
the Bryansford Service Station geography. The record is archived rather
than live — no current voice to quote, an empty Used Cars NI profile — so
the inventory cannot grow without the business engaging. Useful work, small
delta.

### 11. The Donard Hotel — a narrow pitch by design

The concept is deliberately a feature proposal, not a rebuild: the hotel
launched its own navy-and-gold site in 2026 with working booking, published
rates and property photography, and the concept's honest argument is one
screen's worth — the Main Street frontage before the destination, the
published rates beside the official handoff. Elevation material exists
(ESTD 1946, the corner turret, the Mournes ahead) but piling theatre onto
the page would weaken the pitch's discipline: this business has current
capability and recent spend, and the concept earns its hearing by being
measured. Brief only if the pitch strategy changes from feature to
flagship.

### 12. The Buck's Head — the business has answered

On raw material alone this would rank near the top: chef Alex Greene and
Bronagh McCormick since April 2024, #1 of 8 Dundrum restaurants on
TripAdvisor, five menus, the Front Bar's stove and the arch-and-antlers
hearth, rooms reopening after refurbishment. It ranks last because the
pipeline state governs the value of the work: the business replied on 27
July 2026 that a new website is already built and launches with the
accommodation. The recorded next action is to stop the redesign pitch and
re-review the concept against the launched site. Until that re-review
happens, an elevation brief polishes a page whose premise may need
rewriting — the one outcome the method exists to prevent is theatre built
on a stale diagnosis.

## Working the list

Take them one at a time, in order, each derived from the inventory per
`docs/the-elevation-method.md` — no register borrowed from a previous brief
by shape alone. When a brief lands, record it in the concept's
`research/concepts/<slug>/` folder, update its entry here, and pin its moves
in the journey suite as they ship.
