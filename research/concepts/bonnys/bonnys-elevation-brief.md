## The shape of this business

Bonny's is a **stay**: static pitches, a cottage, a log pod, seasonal
facilities at the foot of the Mournes with Dundrum Bay in the view. It is not
a high-street meal, not a hotel reserve theatre, not Sunnyholme under another
nameplate. Recognition lives in the family name, 1948, Tullybrannigan /
Dundrum Bay, and the honest scarcity of a working park whose own domain will
not open.
