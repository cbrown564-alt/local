# Shimna Taxis — elevation brief: Railway Street dispatch, ring to ride

Written 14 September 2026 (Europe/London) for the Day 8 guest concept.
Kind: **ride**. Method: `docs/the-elevation-method.md`, shaped like Scopers
(food-bar) and Mourne Cycles (trade) — re-derived from what this taxi firm
actually has, not from hotel dusk or café linen.

**Status: authored with the guest page the same day.** No clone. No CloudAgent.
Pushed straight to master under `shimna-taxis`.

## Organising principle

The concept competes with the live site at [shimnatravel.co.uk](https://shimnatravel.co.uk/)
and with the phone book. A taxi firm’s feed (if any) and a thin brochure site
already say *24 hours* and *airport*. What they cannot hold is **permanence of
place**, **the dispatch ritual**, and **the door on Railway Street** as the
reason you ring this number and not another Newcastle cab.

So:

- Anything the live site already does — service list, 24/365 claim, female
  driver option, card payment — **absorb and stage**, then **hand off honestly**
  to [shimnatravel.co.uk](https://shimnatravel.co.uk/) for anything that moves.
- Anything a brochure cannot hold — the night desk, the numbered door, the
  two lines that answer, the feel of waiting for a saloon on Railway Street —
  is where greatness lives.

## Diagnosis (why a thin page would fail)

A phone + address + “airport transfers” grid fails the swap test: any NI taxi
could wear it. Packed dusk hero with a utility form box is the Dental anti-pattern
for a ride firm — taxis do not book through a web form they do not run. The
live site already lists services; cloning it as cards without Railway Street
ritual adds nothing. Greatness is **the door + the two lines + the night desk
staged**, then a clean handoff.

## Moves

### 1. Door first — 18 Railway Street as the biggest geography

Utility strip carries address + both phones + 24 hrs / 365. Hero puts
**Shimna Taxis** as the biggest type, **18 Railway Street · Newcastle** under
it, say-line in travel register. No packed form. Airy first screen — night
paper / amber accent, not a dusk photo overlay with a booking box.

### 2. Stock as a dispatch board, phone-readable

Airport transfers, day & coastal trips, executive / VIP, golf trips & tours,
female driver option, card payment — as a readable board, not a brochure essay.
Each cell is a reason to ring, not a fake product SKU.

### 3. Their mechanism, not ours

Primary action is **ring**: `tel:+442843724100` and `tel:+442843723030`, printed
as on the contact page (**028 437 24100** and **028 4372 3030**). Email
`info@shimnatravel.co.uk` as secondary. No invented Timely / WhatsApp / app.

### 4. Ritual artefact — DispatchCard

One unmovable drawn card: dispatch header, **18 Railway Street**, both lines,
24/365 chip, “Ring to book”. Caption guest-voice (“The dispatch card for 18.”).
Drawing disclosure only in `bannerNote`. Ban “not a photograph of…” in captions.

### 5. Magazine middle in taxi/travel register

Short editorial: reliability as the job; Newcastle locals and visitors; special
trips they already name (hospital, ferry, theatre, school runs) without padding
a thin record. Pull the 24/365 line as furniture, not a slogan wallpaper.

### 6. Honest handoff

Footer and a clear link to [shimnatravel.co.uk](https://shimnatravel.co.uk/) for
whatever stays current on their site. Concept does not pretend to be the live
booking system.
