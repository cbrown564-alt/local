## The shape of this business

A **jar** that travels: what the parent buys is a Saturday at *their* venue — a
hall, a school, a living-room floor — and a count of children. The register is
**the table / the party**, not a shop counter and not a stay. Theatre is the
stuffing moment at its best: machine, heart insert, adoption certificate. They
are not hand stuffed. She comes to you. The concept competes with a Facebook
page (`https://www.facebook.com/Bearnecessitiespartyvenue`); today's offers and
competitions stay there. Elevation means making the party irreplaceable by
another teddy business, without inventing a shop door or a hand-sewn myth.

## The essence

**The birthday is the machine, not a hand-sewn bear.**

The paradox is documented: the experience parents book is mechanical stuffing
plus a heart and a certificate, published as packs with per-child prices, and
the party comes to them. A page that romanticises hand-sewing fails the record.
A page that only lists prices is a flyer any party company could wear.
