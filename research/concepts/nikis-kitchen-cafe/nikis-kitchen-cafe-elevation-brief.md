# Niki's Kitchen Café — Elevation Brief: From Good to Flagship

Written 25 August 2026. Elevating the concept to the standard of Enniskeen, Scopers, and The Buck's Head.

## 1. The Organising Principle

Niki's Kitchen Café (107 Central Promenade) is a vibrant, family-run seaside kitchen sitting directly opposite the Irish Sea.
Facebook already wins at live daily blackboard posts and spontaneous bakes.

**What the concept owns**:
- **Seaside Appetite as Theatre (First Screen)**: The warmth of a seaside kitchen — cast-iron skillet breakfasts, golden soda farls, steaming chowder, and tea mugs catching morning light by the sea wall window.
- **The "Weekday Wonders" Kitchen Pass**: Elevating the £12.50 daily special board into an authentic culinary pass celebrating coastal comfort cooking.
- **Promenade Sense of Place**: Physical presence on the sea wall at 107 Central Promenade with direct views of Dundrum Bay and the Mournes.

## 2. Gaps Against Flagship Examples

- **Enniskeen Gap**: Enniskeen grounds itself in landscape and light; Niki's previous concept used a flat teal bar without conveying the sea air, maritime light, or kitchen warmth.
- **Scopers Gap**: Scopers uses macro food detail and culinary narrative; Niki's previous concept used a flat static SVG chalkboard.
- **Buck's Head Gap**: Buck's Head conveys authentic tavern food; Niki's previous concept lacked culinary depth across morning breakfast and lunch counter trades.

## 3. The Elevation Moves

1. **Seaside Appetite Hero**: High-resolution bespoke photography of a traditional skillet fry with soda farl, potato bread, and tea overlooking the Newcastle promenade.
2. **The Kitchen Pass & Daily Blackboard**: Dynamic presentation of Weekday Wonders (£12.50), seafood chowder, homemade lasagne, and freshly baked scones.
3. **Seaside Promenade Navigation**: Clear walk-in directions to 107 Central Promenade, direct phone call (`028 4372 6777`), and transparent hours handoff to Facebook.
4. **Honest Operational Framing**: Explicit respect for operating hours and walk-in trade without fake online ordering.
