## 1. The shape of this business

A **Bustling Morning Diner & Riverside Café (Diner / Café)** situated at the gateway to Newcastle where the Shimna River meets the sea:

- **What the customer buys**: Hearty, freshly griddled all-day breakfasts (Ulster fry, smashed avocado sourdough, poached eggs, pancake stacks), barista coffee, and outdoor terrace seating overlooking the river.
- **The paradox**: A buzzing walk-in policy with zero reservations required for tables, paired with an organized external catering operation delivering fresh sandwich and scone platters across the district.
- **The register**: Morning warmth, sizzle of the griddle, golden daylight on timber tables, river breeze, and generous Mourne hospitality.

The concept wins by capturing the energetic rhythm of Newcastle mornings: hikers gearing up for the mountains, locals gathering for all-day breakfast, and river terrace coffee in the sea air.

### Move 1: Riverside Morning Atmosphere Hero
Open with a sunlit visual of 2 Main Street and the river terrace: steaming mugs of coffee, sizzling bacon and farls, and views toward the Mourne foothills. The copy celebrates all-day dining without clock restrictions.

### Move 2: The All-Day Kitchen Pass
A structured interactive display of the café's signature morning and lunch staples:
- *The Mourne Morning Fry* — dry-cured bacon, pork sausages, griddled potato farl, soda farl, poached or fried free-range eggs.
- *Brunch & Sourdough Deck* — smashed avocado with chilli flakes, poached farm eggs on toasted country sourdough.
- *Midday Lunch & Soup Counter* — daily homemade vegetable broth, gourmet toasties, slow-roasted sandwich specials.

### Move 3: The River Terrace & Walk-In Guide
A practical guide to seating: riverside terrace tables (weather permitting, dog-friendly), indoor booths, and walk-in protocol.
