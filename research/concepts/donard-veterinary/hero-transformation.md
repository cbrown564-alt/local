# Donard Veterinary Clinic — Logo to Real Life Hero Transformation

Exploration conducted on 12 October 2026.

## Thesis

Donard Veterinary Clinic's physical shopfront and window vinyl at 8 Railway Street, Newcastle, feature a distinctive interlocking three-animal silhouette mark:
- A slate dog lying down facing left with front paws extended.
- A teal domestic cat sitting nestled against the dog's chest/shoulder with its back curved and tail arcing upward.
- A white rabbit sitting upright in the foreground nestled between the cat and dog.

This concept explores progressively morphing this 2D graphic silhouette mark into living, breathing companions settled inside the practice's reception lounge, embodying the clinic's fascia motto: *"Professional · Caring · Compassionate"*.

## Streamlined 9-Frame Progression Sequence

A 9-frame progression was generated to preserve continuity of pose while transitioning from vector silhouette to living photorealism:

1. **Frame 01 — Pure Vector Silhouette**: Minimalist 2D graphic mark in solid slate grey, teal, and white within a clean white oval medallion.
2. **Frame 02 — Soft Rim Lighting & Depth**: Multi-layered paper-cut depth with ambient bevels and soft directional illumination along boundaries.
3. **Frame 03 — Anatomical Definition & Line Art**: Fine engraved lines trace facial features, ear folds, muzzle contours, and paws with soft tonal shading.
4. **Frame 04 — Sculptural Bas-Relief Plaque**: Deeply carved sculptural relief with tactile fur strokes, defined eyes, and rich brand slate/teal palette.
5. **Frame 05 — Organic Fur Emergence**: Forms detach from the plaque into free-standing living animals; delicate real whiskers sprout; moist noses and glossy eyes appear with slate and teal tints.
6. **Frame 06 — Natural Coat Transition**: Brand teal hue transitions into sleek blue-grey Russian Blue coat; dog coat softens into rich charcoal/slate labrador fur.
7. **Frame 07 — Studio Portrait Lighting**: Warm amber catchlights in the dog's eyes, luminous cat eyes, crisp hair definition, and glossy coats under studio lighting.
8. **Frame 08 — Warm Clinic Interior & Depth of Field**: Soft floor shadows, warm depth of field, and relaxed companion posture in a quiet, cozy clinic interior.
9. **Frame 09 — Live Hero Reception Shot**: Full real-life scene settled on the clinic reception mat, with the real motto sign (*"Professional, Caring, Compassionate"*) and morning window light.

## Ambient Dissolve Choreography

Rather than a manual scroll scrubber, the hero transition uses an unhurried ~18-second ambient breathing cycle designed for quiet reassurance:

- **Frame 01 (Logo Mark)**: Holds for **2.5s** to establish identity.
- **Frame 02 (Rim Light)**: Lingers for **1.2s** (with a **0.8s** soft crossfade) so the paper-cut relief registers.
- **Frame 03 (Line Art)**: Lingers for **1.2s** (with a **0.6s** crossfade) revealing the sketched facial contours and eyes.
- **Frame 04 (Bas-Relief)**: Holds for **1.0s** (**0.5s** crossfade) showcasing the sculpted plaque.
- **Frames 05–08 (Coat & Light Transitions)**: Smooth progressive crossfades (~0.7s each) warming into living coats.
- **Frame 09 (Live Reception Hero)**: Rests quietly for **3.5s** on the living trio before gently dissolving back to the graphic logo.
