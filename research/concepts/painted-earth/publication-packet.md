## What the concept is testing
A gift shop and upstairs art gallery whose £700 originals are collection-only, sell through the same Add to Cart button as a £3 card, and stay listed once sold — given a gallery shelf that puts collection and shipping terms before the checkout and a route on from work that has gone.


**Research design task:** Representative original-art retail loop: preserve the shop's identity and existing live filters; lead with familiar product and occasion routes, keep place optional, expose collection-only and shipping-quote terms before handoff, and recover sold work to available work by the same artist or the gallery.

The disagreement was narrow and is recorded here rather than settled silently: the concept cannot show what the shop sells, so on the fifth check's "is this better than what they already have" the live gallery wins on photography. The owner's position — that the drawn tiles are visibly labelled placeholders, that the rest of the loop carries enough value to be read on its own, and that asking an owner to imagine their own imagery in place of a labelled placeholder is a small ask — is consistent with Betty's Better Butters and Douglas & Cromie, both public on the same basis. Reviewed against the live shop at phone and desktop; the 27 July finding that the live collection pages already carry price, artist, product-type and stock filters still holds and the concept states it on the page.
