## 1. The shape of this business

A **Place & Stay Experience (Mountain Basecamp / Lodge)**. It is not a boutique hotel and not an urban youth hostel:

- **What the guest buys**: Instant access to the Mourne Mountains (Slieve Donard trailhead, Glen River, Mourne Wall), a warm social communal base, hot showers, gear drying, and a comfortable bed right by the Irish Sea.
- **The paradox**: A legendary 72-bed outdoor hub with a private self-contained flat ("The Padd"), hosting hiking groups, climbers, university expeditions, and solo coastal walkers, operating with an evening-only check-in desk (5:00pm–9:00pm) and a WordPress site with broken HTTPS.
- **The register**: Rugged mountain adventure, camaraderie, boots-on-the-trail energy, salt-air freshness, and welcoming sanctuary after a long ridge hike.

Google holds 4.7★ from 81 reviews; the WordPress site (`hutthostel.com`) serves basic booking information. The concept wins by establishing The Hutt as the definitive Mourne basecamp — clear accommodation tiers (Dorm Bunks vs Private Apartment), live mountain trailhead proximity, and clear group expedition inquiry paths.
