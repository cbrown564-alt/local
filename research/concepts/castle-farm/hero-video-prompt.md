# Castle Farm — weekly-table motion capability test

## Source frame

`public/media/concepts/castle-farm/castle-farm-hero-video-source.png`

The source is an illustrative possible week's table, not a current box. The
image generator produced four carrots despite the source prompt requesting
three; keep the visible source count consistent during video generation.

## Image-to-video prompt

Create a restrained 5-second, 16:9 hand-painted animation from this source.
Keep the camera, crop, table, window, box and every existing food item fixed.
The single anonymous hand lowers its visible bunch of four carrots into the
open navy box, releases them gently among the existing greens, then withdraws
through the upper-left edge. Animate physically coherent fingers, wrist,
carrots, leaves and contact with the box contents. The leafy tops flex once as
the carrots settle; no other produce moves.

Preserve the editorial paint texture without crawling or turning
photorealistic. Do not add or remove food, duplicate the hand or carrots,
change the meat, eggs or dairy, alter the window light, move the camera, warp
the box, introduce labels, text, packaging, logos, faces or audio. This is a
one-shot placement, not a loop: finish with the hand outside the frame and
hold the final arrangement.

## Settings and acceptance

- Duration: 5 seconds; 16:9; camera locked; motion strength low-medium
- Playback: once, then hold the final frame
- One hand only, with coherent fingers and wrist throughout.
- Four source carrots remain four; nothing duplicates or disappears.
- Only the hand, carrot bunch and contacted leaves move.
- Export with no audio.
