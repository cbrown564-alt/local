## The shape of this business

Harbour House is a **family restaurant on the harbour wall** that also keeps
eight bedrooms looking over Dundrum Bay. People come to eat — seafood and
shellfish specials, a bistro that names vegans and vegetarians, South Prom
Brew Bar for coffee looking at the Irish Sea, Water Edge Terrace & Cocktail
Bar. The rooms are real stock (“Book Table” and “Accommodation” sit as peer
links on the home), but the register is **appetite and harbour**, not
Enniskeen dusk windows or a wedding calendar. Contrast deliberately:

- **Not Dundrum Inn** — inland Main Street, bay-over-the-road inn rhythm.
- **Not Buck's Head** — another Newcastle door with its own story.
- **Not Enniskeen** — country-house hotel atmosphere.
- **Door is the harbour / sea wall** — “built on the sea wall overlooking the
  harbour, the most historic area of Newcastle.”

## The essence

of Dundrum Bay, with eight rooms upstairs if the evening runs long.**

Paradox: it is both a restaurant people book a table for and a small stay —
but if the concept leads with “hotel”, it becomes every coastal B&B. If it
leads with the harbour wall, the seafood, and the Connolly welcome, the rooms
become the honest encore. Recognition: *this door, this wall, this family,
this bay.*
