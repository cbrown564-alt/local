## Kind

Trade/workshop with a retail rack. **Why-its-yours kind: ride.** The visitor
wants their bike back on the trail, or wants the next bike.

## Essence

**A race-grade suspension and workshop shop that now has a Main Street
door.** The site's strongest line is its own: "From World Cups to Grass
Roots events". The new retail company on Main Street (June 2026) is the
change moment.

## Voice, geography, ritual, arc, mark

- **Voice:** short, workshop-floor and direct ("Bikes in, fixed right, back
  on the trail."). Their own claims are quoted or closely paraphrased
  (Motorex, accredited, 47%).
- **Geography:** 54–56 Main Street, Castlewellan. No forest-park claims,
  because none are on their site.
- **Ritual:** the job tag. Workshops tag bikes. The booking is a hang-tag
  (punched hole, clipped corners) with Job, Collection (their own form
  field), Bike, What's it doing and Name and number. It composes an email to
  enquiries@, and the live booking form is linked beside it.
- **Arc:** problem → tag → suspension credibility → bikes → academy → door.
- **Mark:** a lab flask whose liquid line is a shock spring, in signal
  orange. It is drawn and new, and it plays on the name.

## Diagnosis of the live presence

1. The site is Square Online and needs JavaScript. Fetchers and slow phones
   see an empty page, and a consent wall from "1090 partners" comes first.
2. The address and opening hours appear nowhere on the site. The contact
   page is a phone number and a form.
3. The best proof (World Cups, Motorex, factory training) is buried on the
   suspension sub-page.
4. The academy, a genuinely unusual thing for a small-town bike shop, sits
   behind an "APPLY HERE" banner with closed applications.
5. The home page has typos: "ACCESSIORES" and "ADVENURE".

## Moves

1. **The name as the biggest type.** "BIKELAB" is set in Anton at up to
   260px on light concrete, with a drawn coil-over spring beside it. The
   hero is airy, with no photograph and no overlay.
2. **The door.** The utility strip has the address, the four services and
   the phone. The hero CTA books the bike in, with a "ring before you call
   in" line instead of invented hours.
3. **Stock readable on a phone: the Tracker ladder.** Seven verified prices
   step in from first kids' bike to Pro. The handoff sends visitors to the
   live shop for sizes and stock.
4. **The ritual object: the job tag.** The JS composes a mailto, and the
   no-JS fallback is a text/plain mailto post.
5. **The unmovable artifact: the academy ruler.** October–March in six
   columns, with rows for fortnightly classes, a monthly ride and monthly
   coaching. It is drawn as rhythm, not as invented dates.
6. **Editorial texture.** Numbered sections (01–04) in mono, like a workshop
   manual.
7. **Honest handoff.** Every service links to the matching live page on
   therealbikelab.com, and Sources lists the site and Companies House.

## Honesty limits

- No hours, reviews, staff names, race results or rider names.
- The 47% and "fully accredited" claims are theirs, used as written. We
  don't say who accredits them.
- Tracker prices are as shown on 16 Nov 2026, and the page says stock
  changes. The research read-date stays here, not on the page.
- The academy is presented as running this winter, with applications
  closed. We don't invent a next intake.
- The image honesty is in the bannerNote only.
