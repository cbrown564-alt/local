## The essence

**We all live here. The van is already in town when the cooker goes cold.**

The paradox is in their own timeline: Tom was due to follow his father to
catering college and became the man you ring when the cooker dies. The 1984
shop is a supermarket-and-butchery he was determined to own so the family had
something. Forty-plus years later the same family still runs two vans for
deliveries, repairs and servicing. A page that is only a catalogue iframe
fails them. A page that invents review scores fails the record. A page that
clones the RNLI post will be stale in a week.

The recognition move is Scopers' kitchen: *we understood the shop.* Not a
white-goods chain. Not a call-centre engineer passing through. Locals, two
vans, this door.
