# Enniskeen — dawn-atmosphere capability test

## Source frame

`public/media/concepts/hotel-enniskeen/enniskeen-hero-video-source.png`

This is a weather-only edit of the faithful dawn visualisation. It does not
record real weather or activity at the hotel.

## Image-to-video prompt

Create a seamless 6-second, 16:9 atmospheric cinemagraph loop from this source.
Keep the camera, crop, house, turret, weathervane, chimneys, rooflines,
windows, ivy, entrance, planters, lawn, drive, horizon, exposure and dawn sky
pixel-stable. Animate only the two thin mist ribbons: the foreground ribbon
drifts very slowly left to right while the distant ribbon moves slightly more
slowly in the opposite direction. Add a barely perceptible breeze to the
outermost leaves in the top-right corner. Keep all windows dark and all other
vegetation still.

No camera movement, time-lapse, changing sunrise, exposure breathing, glowing
windows, chimney smoke, people, cars, animals, rain, new lights, architecture
changes, ivy crawling, roof warping, heavy fog, text, logos, flicker or audio.
The mist density and placement must return naturally so the first and last
frames match.

## Settings and acceptance

- Duration: 6 seconds; preserve source ratio; camera locked; motion very low
- Playback: seamless loop
- The house and planters remain pixel-stable.
- Mist stays translucent and clear of the façade.
- The sky and window light do not change.
- Export with no audio.
