# Tip Top — Elevation Brief: From Good to Flagship

Written 25 August 2026. Elevating the concept to the standard of Enniskeen, Scopers, and The Buck's Head.

## 1. The Organising Principle

Tip Top (131 Main Street) is Newcastle's iconic heritage confectionery and traditional sweet shop, established in 1937.
It holds over 89 years of nostalgic memories for generations of seaside visitors and locals.

**What the concept owns**:
- **Heritage Confectionery Sensory Theatre (First Screen)**: Rows of gleaming vintage glass sweet jars, amber clove rock, barley sugars, polished dark mahogany counters, and antique brass balance scales.
- **The 1937 Weigh-Out Ritual**: Ordering sweets by the quarter and pound into traditional striped paper bags.
- **The Confectioner's Archive**: Comprehensive index of heritage sweets (Boiled Sweets, Mourne Clove Rock, Toffees & Caramels, Traditional Fudge, Sherbets).
- **Historic High Street Landmark**: A living tribute to seaside nostalgia since 1937.

## 2. Gaps Against Flagship Examples

- **Enniskeen Gap**: Enniskeen honors heritage and architectural permanence; Tip Top previously used flat vector jar drawings with zero nostalgia or glass texture.
- **Scopers Gap**: Scopers celebrates craft and provenance; Tip Top previously reduced 89 years of confectionery history to a plain 3-column list.
- **Buck's Head Gap**: Buck's Head evokes 18th-century coaching inn character; Tip Top lacked the warm, golden amber glow of a vintage sweet shop.

## 3. The Elevation Moves

1. **Heritage Sweet Shop Hero**: High-resolution bespoke photography of vintage glass jars filled with sweets on mahogany counters with brass balance scales and warm ambient lighting.
2. **The "Weigh-Out" Counter Experience**: Interactive weigh-out interface allowing guests to explore traditional quarters/pounds.
3. **The 1937 Heritage Index**: Richly categorized sweet collections with tasting notes and nostalgic stories.
4. **Living High Street Heritage**: Honest 131 Main Street address, Google hours, and walk-in nostalgia.
