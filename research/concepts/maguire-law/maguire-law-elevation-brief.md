## Kind

Care/trade hybrid: a professional service where the visitor arrives with a
problem. **Why-its-yours kind: relief.** The page has to lower the visitor's
worry: this is a real solicitor, this is where they are, and this is how to
start.

## Essence

**A new name, a real roll entry, an address you can post to.** The firm is
three months old and its own domain says "Coming Soon". The only full public
record is the Law Society entry, and it is thorough. The page turns that
record into a front door.

## Voice, geography, ritual, arc, mark

- **Voice:** plain, unhurried and first-person plural ("we will tell you
  straight"). No "expert", "specialist" or "leading". NI solicitors may not
  claim specialism without accreditation, and nothing is verified beyond
  the listing.
- **Geography:** 21E Dundrum Road, Newcastle. It appears as an address only.
  We don't describe the building or the view, because we haven't seen them.
- **Ritual:** the file note. A solicitor's first act is opening a file. The
  visitor's first act here is a staged file note: pick the kind of matter,
  give a name and number, write a line. It composes an email to the
  published address. Nothing is sent by the page.
- **Arc:** worry → which kind of matter → who and where → write or ring.
- **Mark:** a folded brief tied in pink legal tape, drawn for the concept.
  It is the oldest visual shorthand of the profession, and no other concept
  in the set uses it.

## Diagnosis of the live presence

1. The domain is a GoDaddy "Coming Soon" page. Anyone given the web address
   finds nothing.
2. The 28 areas of work exist only as an all-caps register on the Law
   Society site. Nobody searches for "ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS". They search
   for "buying a house solicitor Newcastle".
3. No hours are published, and the page doesn't invent any. The door
   answers "when" with "ring to arrange a time".

## Moves

1. **The name as the biggest type.** "Maguire Law" is set in Young Serif at
   up to 196px, with an airy cream hero and no photograph.
2. **The door.** A three-cell strip answers Where (address, linked to the
   map), When (ring, phone) and Who (Fergal Maguire, on the Law Society
   roll).
3. **Stock readable on a phone: the index of matters.** The 28 listed areas
   are regrouped into ten plain-language lines with dotted leaders, like a
   contents page. Each opens to the exact Law Society wording and has its
   own prefilled mailto. The ten groups cover all 28, and none is added.
4. **The ritual object: the file note.** A ruled card with a pink margin
   line and a "File note" tab. The mechanism is a JS-composed mailto with
   the matter in the subject line. Without JS, the form posts as a
   text/plain mailto.
5. **The unmovable artifact: the envelope.** A drawn envelope addressed
   exactly as the Law Society lists the firm and tied in pink tape. It is
   the one object only this firm can have.
6. **Honest handoff.** The footer Sources link to the Law Society entry and
   the Companies House record, the two live sources of truth.

## Honesty limits

- The solicitor is named only as "Fergal Maguire" / "Fergal James Maguire",
  the solicitor on the Law Society listing and the director at Companies
  House. No biography, years of practice or previous firm.
- No fees, hours, reviews, case results or "no win no fee" language.
- The image honesty (the mark, envelope and tape are drawings) is in the
  bannerNote only. Captions describe the object.
- The Companies House history (renamed from FJM Law Ltd) stays in
  research, not on the page.
