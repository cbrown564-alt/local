## 1. The shape of this business

A **Master Butcher & Hot Carvery Deli (Jar / Counter)** inside Newcastle Shopping Centre:

- **What the customer buys**: Prime locally sourced dry-aged beef, artisan house sausages, oven-ready meat trays, and freshly carved hot lunchtime carvery rolls (roast pork/beef, stuffing, and rich gravy).
- **The paradox**: A traditional butchery counter upholding artisanal dry-aging and carcass butchery, combined with a fast-paced hot lunch deli serving locals and workers daily.
- **The register**: Deep burgundy meat tones, marble counters, heavy end-grain chopping blocks, hot roasting aromas, and direct master butcher craft.

The concept wins by uniting the traditional butchery craft (the weekend roast or barbecue pack) with the daily lunchtime carvery ritual that draws returning queues.
