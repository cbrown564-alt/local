## The shape of this business

Morelli's is Ireland's famous Italian ice-cream family — Coleraine 1911,
centenary 2011, Champion of Champions vanilla, scoop shops and stockists
across the island. **This concept is only the Newcastle promenade door.**
The customer buys a cone or a cup and walks the Central Promenade with the
Mournes behind and the sea beside. Theatre register is **the melt / the walk**,
Betty's-table register adapted to a promenade: the product at the moment it
is for — not a restaurant booking arc, not a Portrush HQ documentary.

Contrast / anti-patterns:

- Not Café Mauds (meal + waffle room that also serves ice cream).
- Not a factory-tour or wholesale story.
- Not “every Morelli's in Ireland” on one page.
- Not an invented delivery / order form for this door.

## The essence

**The Morelli scoop on Newcastle's Central Promenade — same famous ice cream,
this seafront door.**

Paradox: a century brand that must feel like one kiosk. A concept that tells
the full Morelli novel fails the prom. A concept that pretends this is an
independent gelateria fails the name on the awning. Recognition: *73 Central
Promenade, a cone, the walk.*

## The diagnosis

1. **Brand gravity.** Easy to write Portrush/Coleraine and forget the prom.
2. **Address drift.** 73 (first-party) vs 75 / unnumbered Central Promenade
   in directories — prefer 73; note the conflict in research.
3. **Hours vacuum on first-party list page.** Inventing 8–10 as fact fails
   honesty; omitting hours entirely may fail usefulness — dated third-party
   or “hours on the door / ask” are the safe moves.
4. **Order-form temptation.** National site is not a Newcastle cart. No fake
   “Order now”.
5. **Jar/meal confusion.** Do not build a full café journey (breakfast,
   brunch, book a table). Scoop + walk.

## The moves

### 1. Name the Newcastle door in biggest type

“Morelli's @ Newcastle” / Central Promenade — their buy-list wording. 73 and
BT33 0HH from first-party.

### 2. Theatre in the jar/prom register

Hero is the cone or cup **in motion toward the prom** — melt, light, sea air
implied — not a dining room. Betty's melt logic: the product at the moment it
is for. Generated imagery disclosed; no pretending it is a photo of this
till's staff.

### 3. One heritage line, then stop

Optional: “Morelli's ice cream — the family name since 1911” with handoff to
morellisices.com — or simply the scoop. Do not serialise the wartime internment
story on a guest cone page.

### 4. Honest action: come to the prom

CTA is wayfinding + open invitation, not a form. Map pin / address / “on the
Central Promenade”. No delivery widget.

### 5. Hours with a provenance tag — or omit

If hours ship: “Directories list daily 8am–10pm — confirm on the door”
dated 13 Sep 2026. Better: omit until first-party or a labelled Google read
is accepted in Sources.

### 6. Sister discipline

Other Morelli's doors (Portrush, Portstewart, Belfast…) stay off the guest
page except a single “find other shops on morellisices.com” handoff if
needed.

### 7. Address honesty

Print **73**. Mentions of 75 stay in research NOTES as conflict, not guest
copy.
