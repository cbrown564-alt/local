## The organising principle

Facebook already wins at *now*. 1.7K followers. Reels to 1K views. The one
readable post, 14 August 2025: *"Newcastle primary 1/4 zips now in stock-
who's going to be first to get one?"* The grid is schoolwear and suits. Intro:
*"Men's designer clothing, men's wedding and formal hire, boys' and girls'
school uniforms, men's footwear."* Page name: Binghams Menswear Formal Hire.

The site already holds the catalogue the feed cannot: hire garment names, sale
suits from £159, school-by-school list, brand logos, three appointment routes.
It also holds the decay: `shop.binghamsmenswear.com` is 404 "This store is
unavailable" under a "Visit Our Online Store" headline; `/highland-wear/`
404s while Highland is a home tile and a form field; fitting page still says
*"Covid 19 social distancing practises are in place"* and *"Please do not
book an appointment for before Wednesday 1st July."* Images stop January
2022. Footer © 2026. No hours. Three emails in public. Facebook lists
binghamsmenswear.**co.uk**; the live host is **.com**.

So:

- **Anything the feed already does well — this week's quarter-zip, the reel —
  hand off honestly.**
- **Anything a feed cannot hold — the appointment, the school list, hire as
  permanence without fake prices — is where greatness lives.** Do not rebuild
  a shop that 404s. Do not freeze hire prices they never published.

## The essence

**Come in for the occasion. The internet is not the shop — and their internet
still asks you not to book before 1 July.**

The paradox is a working high-street door (82 Main Street, 028 4372 3521) with
a site that performs 2020 and a feed that performs this term. A concept that
only lists Benetti and Tommy Hilfiger is any menswear page. A concept that
invents hire packages fails the record. Recognition: *we understood the
counter* — wedding / formal / school, half-hour fittings, full range only
in-store.
