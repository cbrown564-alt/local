## The organising principle

Facebook already wins at this week's operations. The one readable post, dated
about 20 August 2026 ("3d" on 23 August), is the whole current argument:

> will be operating by appointment only while the builders are in.\nPlease
> call us on 02843722991 for any emergencies, repairs or collections.\nThank
> you for your understanding. We look forward to showing you the new and
> updated practice soon!"

The photo grid is stock and finish: Lazer sunglasses £99 including
prescription; Polaroid kids prescription sunglasses £19; 20% off this week;
Flash Sale 20% off designer and branded glasses; WOLF Eyewear cases; frames
on a rack; handovers in branded cloths. 368 followers, 13 reviews at 100%
recommend, one readable line. Reels exist (one counted 716 views).

The practice's own site is a splash, copyright frozen at 2022: logo,
"-Established 1983-", Railway Street, the phone, the .com. `armstrongopticians.co.uk`
is a GoDaddy lander, not them. There is no email, no hours, no service list,
no staff, no booking, no hearing mention on either first-party surface.

So:

- **Anything the feed already does well — the refit, this week's 20% off,
  the new frames — hand off honestly.** Those posts will be stale in a
  fortnight. Do not freeze "appointment only" as if it were the practice
  forever, and do not rebuild a sale board.
- **Anything a feed cannot hold — who they have been since 1983, what
  "enhanced eye care and unique eyewear" means as a door, how a stranger
  gets a diary slot, what to do when vision goes suddenly — is where
  greatness lives.**

The raw assets: a creed in their words; a founding year on their own splash;
a Railway Street door; one phone; a refit-era emergency line that is *ring
us*, not A&E; one parent-plain review; flyer prices that are dated offers,
not a tariff. The raw *absence*: no named optometrist, no NHS/private split,
no hours on their pages, no hearing tenant on any first-party page.

## The essence

**Independent since 1983. One door on Railway Street. The diary is the phone.**

The paradox we *had* (two trades, one building) is not on their record. The
paradox we *have* is smaller and truer: a practice that has been on this
street since 1983 still answers the web with a 2022 splash and a Facebook
that walls a stranger after one post — while this week they are under
builders and will only see you if you ring. A page that invents a hearing
partner, an NHS menu, or hours copied from Bing is a different business. A
page that only says "call us" is any high-street optician. Recognition is
Scopers' kitchen move: *we understood the practice* — independent, enhanced
eye care, unique eyewear, established 1983, appointment by phone, Railway
Street.

## The moves

### 1. Their voice, not ours — the Intro, large

Cheapest move, and the one that makes the swap test bite.

### 2. Established 1983, this door, this number

second number. Do not add "Armstrong & Armstrong" unless they use it.

### 3. The diary is the phone — and this week's truth is appointment-only

Desk note: who, when you hope to come, a return number. Words for the call.
Nothing sends. No email to invent. A dated, dismissible band for the refit
(*appointment only while the builders are in* — their words, ~20 August 2026)
that is allowed to come off the moment a later read shows the floor open
again. Do not let a temporary notice become the identity.

### 4. Emergency, in two registers

Practice emergencies, repairs, collections: their number (their words).
Sudden loss of vision: A&E or 111 as public guidance, not as Armstrong
speech. Never "ring us first" for that.

### 5. Theatre — frames and a case, not a room we have not seen

**Creative fork:** (a) keep the AI calm room, disclosed; or (b) first-screen
on what the grid actually contains — a handover, a WOLF case, the rack —
generated riff, triple disclosure. Recommendation: (b) beside "unique
eyewear"; calm room only if we are staging *enhanced eye care* and we say it
is not a photograph of Railway Street. After the refit they may supply a real
interior. That replaces both.

### 6. Offers live on Facebook — **handoff, not a move to clone**

One line: this week's frames and sunglasses are on Facebook. Do not typeset
£99 / £19 / 20% as if they were standing prices.

### 7. Hearing — unshipped on purpose

If a later first-party page names The Hearing Care Partnership at 30 Railway
Street with a number, *then* the two-door layout earns itself. Until that
read, the building is one practice.
