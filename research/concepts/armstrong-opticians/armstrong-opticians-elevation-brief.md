# Armstrong Opticians — elevation brief: the practice being refurbished everywhere except its front door

Rewritten 23 August 2026 (second pass, evening). The morning pass got the
honesty right — it caught the two-door hearing error, the A&E-as-their-words
error, the 2013 founding-year trap — and then stopped there. Its moves were
mostly removals, its theatre was an unresolved fork, and it never named the
thing the harvest actually shows. This pass keeps the harvest, keeps every
honesty constraint, and re-derives the elevation from the record's real
centre of gravity. It supersedes the morning brief in full.

Sources, all read 23 August 2026: logged-out re-read of
`https://www.facebook.com/Armstrongopticians` (Intro, About, photos, one
reachable post, one readable review, then the login wall); live read of
`https://armstrongopticians.com` (single GoDaddy-era splash); first-party

028 4064 8306.

The general method is `docs/the-elevation-method.md`. The shape is **care**
(care register from Donard Veterinary and Newcastle Dental: reassurance, not

ritual is where the theatre lives.

## The insight the first pass missed

The harvest has exactly one reachable post, and the first pass treated it as
an operational notice to be tolerated and dismissed. Read it again:

> "We are getting a refresh! The big practice refit has started today, so we
> will be operating by appointment only while the builders are in … We look
> forward to showing you the new and updated practice soon!"

Now set it beside the practice's own website: a one-page splash whose
copyright line froze at 2022, carrying a founding line — "-Established
1983-" — and nothing else.

The practice is, in the same month, **43 years old and under the builders**.
It is investing in new rooms while its digital front door is the stalest
thing it owns. And — the part that matters — they promised, in their own
words, to *show* people the new practice. The only place they can currently
show anything is a Facebook page that walls a stranger after one post.

That is the pitch, whole: **the practice is getting a refresh, and its front
door on the web never got one.** The concept is the parallel refurbishment —
the showing they promised, running ahead of the builders. It is recognition
no template can fake, because it starts from the only post the practice has
published this month.

The second thing the first pass weighed and set down: the photo grid is not
clinical. It is *choosing* — frames on racks, WOLF cases, branded handover
cloths, kids' sunglasses at £19, a flash 20% off. "Enhanced eye care and
unique eyewear" is the creed; the grid is the evidence that the lived ritual
of this practice is the wall of frames and the handover. Theatre belongs to
that ritual, not to a generic calm room that could be any clinic in any town.

## The essence

**Same door since 1983. New rooms this autumn. The diary is still the phone.**

The paradox is documented, not invented: the oldest thing about the practice
(1983, their own splash) and the newest (the refit, their only reachable
post) in one breath. A page that says only "call us" is any high-street
optician. A page that invents hours, clinicians, an NHS menu or a hearing
tenant is a different business. The elevated page is the one that says: *we
read the one post you have, and we built the showing you promised.*

## The diagnosis

Against this harvest and the shipped `src/concepts/armstrong-opticians/home.astro`:

1. **The refit is on the page but not *used*.** It sits in a dismissible
   strip as an inconvenience notice, when it is the only post the practice
   has, the recognition anchor, and the pitch itself. The page never says
   the interesting sentence: this practice is being renewed everywhere except
   here.
2. **"Established 1983" is a kicker, not an arc.** Forty-three years and a
   refit is a history band at exactly the scale the record allows — two
   documented pins plus a third the practice wrote itself ("soon"). The
   history-told-properly move costs nothing here and is currently unmade.
3. **The calm-room plate is any clinic.** Cool light, a chair, a slit of
   window: it fails the swap test. The grid says the practice's theatre is
   the frame wall and the handover — and this month, honestly, the dust
   sheets. The plate is theatre in the wrong room.
4. **No keepable artifact.** A diary-by-phone practice hands you one thing
   in real life: the appointment card. The record carries exactly the four
   facts a card needs (name, street, number, 1983) — the artifact is buildable
   with zero invention.
5. **No photographic voice.** One plate, no grade.

## The moves

Ordered by build sequence: voice first, then the essence made visible, then
the artifact, then theatre.

### 1. Their voice, not ours — the Intro, large *(shipped; keep)*

The Intro stays as hero furniture, Facebook findable. Cheapest move, and the
one that makes the swap test bite.

### 2. The refit notice stays a notice — but dated and theirs *(shipped; keep)*

The dismissible band with their words and its ~20 August 2026 date stands.
What changes is everything around it: the page stops treating the refit as an
interruption and starts treating it as the story (moves 3 and 4). The band
must still be allowed to die when the builders leave — a temporary notice is
not the identity.

### 3. The essence made visible: the two-and-a-half-pin arc *(this pass)*

New section. Kicker "The practice". Heading and pins:

> **Same door since 1983.**
>
> **1983** — The practice opens on Railway Street.
> **August 2026** — The builders come in. Appointment only while the work is
> done.
> **Soon** — "We look forward to showing you the new and updated practice
> soon."

Lead paragraph, in the practice's voice:

> Independent optometrists on Railway Street since 1983. This autumn the
> rooms are being remade — the same door, the same number, and a new
> practice at the end of it.

Every pin is sourced: the splash, the refit post, the refit post. The third
pin is the charm — the only future-dated entry is the practice's own promise,
quoted. Newcastle Dental's timeline rule applies: dates only where
documented, sources in research and Sources & limits, light attribution on
the guest layer.

### 4. Theatre: the frame wall, mid-refresh *(mechanism now, asset on generation)*

Retire the calm room as the hero register. Spec for the generation session:
**the choosing wall at an independent optician's, mid-refit** — a wall of
frames half-draped in a dust sheet, cases on the counter, warm work light;
or, if that reads as untidy rather than honest, **the choosing wall straight**
— rows of frames, a WOLF case, a branded handover cloth, morning light.
Either is built from what the grid actually contains; both are disclosed
generated imagery, never captioned as a photograph of Railway Street. The
hero composition, veil, grade hook and disclosure ship now so the swap is
one asset. The calm-room plate may stay down-page beside the emergency strip,
where calm is the right register, or retire — decide at generation.

### 5. The keepable artifact: the appointment card *(this pass)*

A drawn appointment card — CSS/SVG, no generation — set beside the desk
note, because the diary-by-phone practice's one physical artifact is the card
they write your appointment on. Carries only sourced facts: **Armstrong
Opticians · 30 Railway Street, Newcastle · 028 4372 2991 · Established
1983**, and one blank ruled line: *Your appointment*. No instruction to ask
for one (the record does not say cards exist); no invented branding. Kicker
"For the diary"; lead: **"Everything you need fits on one card."** It is the
one-sheet's front when a sheet is printed, and it gives the owner the
keepable thing without inventing a service menu.

### 6. The desk note is the ritual *(shipped; keep)*

Who needs seen, when you hope to come, a number they can return; the words
for the call; nothing sent. Right mechanism, right register.

### 7. Offers and emergencies, two honest handoffs *(shipped; keep)*

This week's frames live on Facebook; sudden loss of vision is A&E / 111 as
public guidance, never the practice's voice. Both stand.

### 8. One photographic voice — the practice grade *(this pass)*

A shared, composable `--ao-grade` custom property over every plate (the
Enniskeen lesson: chain, don't override), so current and future plates read
as one commission — warm-neutral, eased saturation, soft shadow lift.
