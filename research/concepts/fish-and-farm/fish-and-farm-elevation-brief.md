## 1. Kind and the shape of this business

**Kind: jar** — a thing you take home.

Fish & Farm is Alex Greene and Bronagh McCormick’s retail kitchen at
**18 Main Street, Newcastle, BT33 0AD**: prepared fish and farm dishes for
the table at home, with a 2024 deli sit-in for coffee and light bites.
Opened **2019**; relaunched **January 2024** with the deli counter. Sister
door: **The Buck’s Head** (Dundrum) — shared gift vouchers, different kind
(meal / stay), different action (book a table).

- **What the customer buys**: Lasagnes, pies, chowder, soups, sausage rolls,
  breads, and locally sourced fresh fish prepared in-house; plus deli scones,
  salad bowls, breakfast toasties, croissants, mini pies, tray bakes, tea and
  coffee — sit-in or takeaway.
- **The paradox**: A Michelin-starred / GBM chef’s kitchen that sells as a
  **take-home counter**, not as a restaurant reserve — the banquet skill
  wrapped for your fridge, while the sister door holds the white tablecloth.
- **The register**: Atlantic fish, farm cream, copper ticket clip, butcher
  paper, kitchen-pass type. Warm, practical, Main Street — not dusk hotel
  overlay, not sweet-shop raspberry, not bakery flour dust.

Wrong kind = fail. Do not build a Buck’s Head booking page, a Tip Top jar
wall, or a Cookie Jar morning-loaf deck.
