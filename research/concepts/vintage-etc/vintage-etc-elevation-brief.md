## 1. The shape of this business

A **Curated Antiques & Mid-Century Trove (Jar / Curios)** on Newcastle Main Street:

- **What the customer buys**: High-character vintage furniture (1950s–1970s G-Plan and Danish teak sideboards), maritime brass curios, retro barware, and collectible original-pressing vinyl records.
- **The paradox**: Fast-moving, one-of-a-kind inventory where items posted in the morning are often collected by the evening, operating entirely without a generic e-commerce cart.
- **The register**: Aged teak grain, polished brass, vinyl crackle, warm mid-century nostalgia, and tactile discovery.

The concept wins by pairing the excitement of the physical shop floor discovery with a swift "Hold a Piece" telephone reservation line for pieces spotted on daily social feeds.
