# Newcastle Family Dental Care — elevation brief: the award-winning practice that lost its own door

Written 4 August 2026, third in the ranked sequence in
`docs/elevation-brief-priorities.md`. The general method is in
`docs/the-elevation-method.md`; this is the dental-practice application of
it. The Care register was first worked out in
`research/concepts/donard-veterinary/donard-veterinary-elevation-brief.md` —
the register transfers, the moves do not; everything below is derived from
this practice's own record.

**Status: written 4 August 2026; not yet implemented.** The current concept
is an honest security case — the padlocked address bar, "Your family
dentist, close to home", the three named dentists, a request panel — and it
passes the publication checks. It is also entirely typographic and entirely
studio-voiced. The re-read for this brief found the practice's own former
website in the Internet Archive (live to May 2019), and with it the
practice's own words and its proudest fact — none of which is on the page.

## The shape of this business

A **care** business: what the patient buys is emotion under management —
the appointment they have been putting off, the child’s first visit, the
tooth that finally hurts too much. The register is **reassurance**, and the
theatre must feel like relief, not spectacle. The concept's existing thesis
— the practice's own front door, served securely — is the right spine: this
is the only prospect where the digital failure has a patient-privacy
dimension. Elevation here means the door gets the practice's own voice,
history and warmth back onto it, without widening the pitch beyond the door.

## The essence

**The practice that was named Dental Practice of the Year has no website of
its own.**

The paradox is documented, not invented: the practice's own archived site
announces the Randox Healthcare Dental Practice of the Year award for 2017
and the BDA Good Practice Scheme — and today a patient who types the
practice's own domain is handed, over plain HTTP, to a single location page
on a Portadown group's site that mentions none of it. Award-winning care,
homeless identity. The concept already restores the door; the elevation
puts the nameplate, the history and the welcome back on it.

One caveat frames everything: the commercial relationship between the
practice and the DJ Maguire group is **unestablished** (group membership,
ownership or hosting — the record cannot say), and the group lists the
practice among its twelve locations. The concept speaks to patients about
the Newcastle practice and claims nothing, in either direction, about who
owns it.

## What recognition can draw on

The record now has two layers: the **current published layer** (the group's
Newcastle location page, read 4 August 2026) and the **archived layer** (the
practice's own site, captured by the Internet Archive through May 2019).
Every use of the archived layer must be dated and attributed; it is the
practice's own voice, but it is not its current voice.

- **Their voice:** current — "New Online Service: we are now offering you
  the option to make a private appointment to help you with your dental
  care needs." Archived — "We are a modern friendly family orientated
  practice offering NHS, Private and Cosmetic dentistry"; "We provide high
  quality, ethical dentistry in a caring environment"; and the line that
  passes the best-sentence test: **"We love to make you smile!"**
- **Their people:** three named dentists on the current page — Dr David
  Knowles (BDS QUB 2014), Dr Rebecca McCarey (BDS QUB 2014) and Dr Daniel
  Sandford (Grado en Odontología UCV 2023). The archived team page shows
  Knowles and McCarey — with GDC numbers 250740 and 250716 — graduated the
  same year at the same school and still practise together: the partnership
  is the practice's spine. Sandford's 2023 arrival is the practice still
  growing.
- **Their arc:** "established for many years" with two surgeries "equipped
  to the highest standards"; **Dental Practice of the Year 2017** (Randox
  Healthcare) — "What a start to 2017 for the team" — and the BDA Good
  Practice Scheme award; their own site until at least May 2019; the domain
  later falling to the insecure redirect the concept is built on.
- **Their ritual:** welcoming patients back — the archived site explicitly
  invited "patients that have never visited the practice at all or previous
  patients that have not attended for some time" — and the emergency
  routine: in hours, "contact the practice asap"; out of hours, call the
  same number and listen to the recorded message. The recorded-message
  protocol is archived-era only and must not be published as current
  without first-hand re-verification.
- **Their geography:** one address, honestly — Iveagh Court, 2 Railway
  Street, Newcastle, BT33 0SP. No catchment list is published, so no
  catchment map is drawn.
- **Their mark:** the practice name itself; no logo file is held in either
  layer. The concept's drawn tooth mark is studio chrome and stays modest.

## The diagnosis

1. **No sentence on the page is theirs.** Every line is studio copy. For a
   concept whose whole argument is "this practice deserves its own voice",
   the absence of the practice's actual voice is the central gap — and the
   archive supplies it.
2. **The award is nowhere.** The proudest documented fact of the practice
   exists only in a 2018 capture. The current group page does not mention
   it; the concept does not mention it. Recognition begins here.
3. **The partnership story is unused.** Two dentists who graduated together
   in 2014 and still practise together, joined by a third in 2023 — the
   team cards carry names and credentials but not the arc.
4. **The page's one interactive element is inert.** "Send request securely"
   is a placeholder anchor. The portfolio already has the honest pattern for
   this — the reviewed mailto draft — and an inert send button is the
   strikethrough-shelf in a new costume.
5. **Zero warmth.** The publication record says it plainly: the page is
   entirely typographic. Reassurance cannot be typography alone; it needs
   one drawn, human-scale moment.

## The moves

Ordered by the recommended build sequence: voice first, then the essence
made visible, then theatre.

### 1. Give the practice its own sentence back

Open the welcome on **"We love to make you smile!"** — quoted verbatim,
attributed to the practice's own website with its archived date, so a
reader can tell it is the practice's historic words rather than the
studio's. Support it with the current layer's own line about private
appointments ("help you with your dental care needs"), and keep "Your
family dentist, close to home" as the studio headline if it earns its
place beside theirs — their words lead, ours frame.

### 2. Put the award and the arc on the door

A short **The practice** band, dates only where documented:

- **2017** — named Randox Healthcare Dental Practice of the Year; awarded
  the BDA Good Practice Scheme (from the practice's own site, archived
  2018, and dated so no one reads a nine-year-old award as this year's)
- **2014** — Drs Knowles and McCarey graduate together at Queen's; they
  still practise together here
- **2023** — Dr Sandford joins the practice
- **Today** — three dentists, two surgeries, Monday to Friday 9–5, one
  phone number

Every entry carries its source and its read or capture date. The owner —
one of those three dentists, or the practice manager, reading on a phone
between patients — sees the studio found the fact their current one-page
hosting never mentions. That is the recognition move.

### 3. Make the emergency path a calm mechanism

The Care register's theatre is reassurance, and this practice publishes
the bones of one: in hours, ring the practice; out of hours, the same
number carried the instructions. The honest current rendering, matching
what the page already says: **In hours: call 028 4372 3776 and we will fit
you in as soon as we can. Out of hours: call 111.** Present it as a simple
two-step mechanism panel — what happens when you ring — rather than a
footnote under the form. Do not publish the archived recorded-message
protocol as current; it is an open decision below, not a move.

### 4. Draw the calm room

The page needs one human-scale, obviously-drawn moment in the concept's
periwinkle: an illustrated plate of the calm room on Railway Street — the
chair by the window, the plant, the quiet — disclosed as illustration. Not
photoreal: generated photoreal dentistry would read as the real practice,
which is the wrong side of the honesty line, exactly as generated pets
were for the vet. If the room plate reads as invented interior on review,
fall back to a drawn Iveagh Court doorway plate. This is the reassurance
theatre: the room a nervous patient is being asked to walk into, set at
its calmest.

### 5. Make the request panel work the honest way

Replace the inert "Send request securely" with the portfolio's reviewed
pattern: name, best number and reason (Register / Check-up / In pain)
prepare a draft email to the published `info@newcastlefamilydentalcare.co.uk`
address, opened in the patient's own mail app — nothing is sent from the
page, and the panel says so in its three on-page labels, with the draft
withdrawn if the details change. This makes the page's one claim of
function true, keeps "No details shared off-site" literally accurate, and
routes every new section back to the same honest handoff rather than
implying online booking.

## Recommended bundles

- **First pass:** moves 1, 2 and 5 — their sentence, the award band, the
  working request. All from the record; no new assets; it turns the page
  from a studio poster into the practice's own door.
- **Theatre pass:** moves 3 and 4 — the mechanism panel and the calm-room
  plate.
- **No map for this one.** The record supports a single address and no
  published catchment; a drawn catchment would be invented geography. The
  keepable-artifact slot stays empty until the practice supplies more.

## Honesty constraints

- **Two layers, never blended.** Current facts: the three dentists, Iveagh
  Court, Mon–Fri 9–5, the phone and email, the "New Online Service" private
  appointments line. Everything else — the award, the smile line, the GDC
  numbers, NHS availability, late and early hours, the recorded-message
  protocol — is archived-era and must be dated, attributed, and never
  presented as current.
- **Say nothing about NHS availability today.** The 2018 site welcomed new
  NHS patients; the current page mentions only private appointments. The
  triage record already removed unsourced NHS copy once. The concept
  neither promises nor denies NHS places.
- **Say nothing about the group relationship.** No claim of independence,
  no claim of membership. The practice is presented as itself, at its
  address, with its own people.
- **No clinical claims and no invented treatments.** The care list stays at
  the published items (new patients, private appointments, family & general
  dentistry, Mon–Fri 9–5); cosmetic, sedation, implants and the rest of the
  group's service list are the group's words, not the Newcastle practice's,
  and stay off the page.
- **No invented reviews.** None exist anywhere in the record; the pull-quote
  is the practice's own sentence, not a patient's.
- **The padlock stays paired with the banner note**, per the publication
  record: it is concept chrome, not a live certificate, and the two must
  never be separated by a layout change.
- Health context keeps the accessibility bar high: real labels, big
  targets, focus visible, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, honest text
  over cleverness at the real 390px viewport.
- Any change to the first viewport recaptures the comparison stills and
  clip in the same commit, per `docs/MEDIA_CAPTURE.md`.

## Tests that pin the elevation

- Assert the smile line reaches the page with its attribution and archived
  date, and that the award line carries "2017" and its source — no
  undated glory.
- Assert every arc entry carries its date, and no archived-era claim
  (NHS places, late nights, recorded message) appears presented as current.
- Assert the request panel drafts an email to
  `info@newcastlefamilydentalcare.co.uk` carrying name, number and reason,
  that nothing else sends, and that editing a detail withdraws the prepared
  draft.
- Assert the emergency panel shows the two honest steps and no unsourced
  promise (no "seen the same day", no Sunday line).
- Assert the padlock chrome and the banner note survive together in the
  rendered first viewport.
- If the calm-room plate lands, assert its illustration disclosure in alt
  text, on the page and in the case study, and inspect it at 390px.
- Run the guest-voice guard and the publication checks; then the swap test:
  remove "Newcastle Family Dental Care", the award and the three dentists,
  and if the page still works for any practice, the elevation has not gone
  far enough.

## Open decisions

- Is the archived smile line the right lede quote, given the practice no
  longer publishes it? Attributed and dated, it is honest; the alternative
  is the current "help you with your dental care needs", which is weaker.
  Decide at review.
- Do the two published GDC numbers (250740, 250716) belong on the team
  cards? They are the practice's own published credentials, but Dr
  Sandford's is not in the record, so the row would be uneven. Include
  with dates, or omit entirely.
- Should the recorded-message out-of-hours protocol ever be published?
  Only after someone has rung the practice line out of hours and heard it
  first-hand; until then the 111 line stands.
- The calm-room plate versus waiting for a practice-supplied photograph —
  the publication record already names the photograph as the first
  improvement on engagement; the plate is the honest bridge until then.

## When a move ships

Record the source and read or capture date for every new fact — archived
facts cite the Wayback capture, current facts cite the read date — add
provenance for any new asset in the same commit, update the case-study
Sources & limits block, and add journey coverage for new behaviour. Any
move that changes the first viewport recaptures the comparison stills and
clip in the same commit. Update this status line as moves land; do not
call the concept validated until an owner or representative visitor has
supplied that evidence.
