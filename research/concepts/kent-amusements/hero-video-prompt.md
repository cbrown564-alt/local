# Kent Amusements — promenade-motion capability test

## Source frame

`public/media/concepts/kent-amusements/kent-amusements-hero-video-source.png`

The figures are anonymous generated illustrations. The arcade and promenade
remain indicative rather than an architectural survey or recorded event.

## Image-to-video prompt

Create a natural 6-second, 16:9 animated-illustration shot from this source.
Keep the camera, crop, architecture, road, railings, sea, mountains, machines,
blank fascia panels, ink linework, gouache fills and paper texture completely
fixed. The anonymous adult and child walk together from right to left at a
gentle pace toward the open arcade, remaining seen from behind. Preserve their
handhold throughout. The child's red balloon trails slightly behind, making
one soft wind-driven sway while its string stays attached and taut. Add only a
very small shimmer on the sea and slight cloud drift; do not animate the arcade
machines or invent flashing signage.

Maintain coherent walking anatomy, scale and illustration style. No camera
motion, cuts, morphing architecture, crowds, new people, cars, animals,
readable lettering, logos, dramatic waves, photorealism, texture crawling,
flicker or audio. This is a one-shot entrance, not a loop: end with both
figures mostly concealed by the arcade doorway and hold the final frame.

## Settings and acceptance

- Duration: 6 seconds; 16:9; camera locked; motion strength low-medium
- Playback: once, then hold the final frame
- The figures retain normal limbs and a stable handhold.
- The balloon remains one object with one attached string.
- The façade and blank panels do not warp or acquire text.
- Export with no audio.
