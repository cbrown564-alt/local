## Objection notes

### "We already have a booking engine."

Yes - the concept keeps it. The availability bar passes arrival and nights
into the same Bookin1 results route the current site uses. The proposed work is
the hotel-owned journey around the engine, not a replacement reservation
system.

### "We cannot afford an agency rebuild."

The next step does not have to be a rebuild. Paid work begins only after a
fixed written quote, and the first scope can stay small - one useful page or
one booking-journey improvement. Build, domain and hosting costs are separated
and there is no forced long-term subscription.

### "Those are our photographs."

They are, and the case study says so prominently. The concept reuses the
hotel's publicly available photographs to show that the existing material is
already strong. They would be removed immediately on request, and any live
project would use only assets the hotel authorises.

### "The concept was not commissioned."

Correct. Every concept page, the film and the one-sheet label it as independent
and not commissioned. It is a concrete proposal to judge, not a claim of a
client relationship or approval.

### "We are happy with the current site."

That is a complete answer. The concept can remain a private example or be
removed. The useful question is whether guests can see the rooms, dining and
estate clearly before they reach Bookin1; if the present route already serves
the hotel well enough, no change is required.
