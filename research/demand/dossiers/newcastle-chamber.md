## What we know

Sourced from `research/pipeline/verifications.json`,
`research/concepts/newcastle-chamber/` and the July audit.

- No website: a Gmail address, Facebook and Instagram. At least eight
  same-named chambers worldwide crowd its name in search.
- A volunteer committee, reformed in 2023. Officer names in our record
  are unconfirmed snippet data.
- The July audit: "active in local programming, yet no strong owned
  platform with a trusted directory, offers, news and visitor-facing
  commercial information … A good shared platform may outperform selling a
  large site to every microbusiness."

## Why it's on the list

The Chamber isn't a revenue prospect. It is in the ten because:

1. **It's a channel.** Its members are the businesses this studio serves.
   One committee conversation reaches more doors than ten cold visits, and
   a members' evening is a room full of the lean-presence businesses we
   just archived, who may want something smaller.
2. **It tests the audit's shared-platform idea.** Many of the 26 archived
   walk-in businesses need an accurate listing, not a site. A trusted
   directory could be that listing.
3. **PRODUCT.md's promise:** paid work funds time for clubs and
   organisations that can't stretch to a commercial project.

## The problem we think they have

Visitors can't find a town directory or what's on; traders can't see why
to join; the committee has no place to point either of them.

## Would they want it?

- **For:** no site at all; a real public job to do.
- **Against:** a volunteer committee has no budget, little time, and a
  directory needs someone to keep it current. An unmaintained directory is
  exactly what the audit criticised in `newcastle-county-down.com`.
- **Kill question:** "Who on the committee would keep a member list
  current, and how often does it change?"

## Pitch to test

> "You've got the events and the members; you don't have one place to send
> people. I built a version of that as a community contribution. What would
> it need to be useful to you, and who would keep it right?"

## Smallest scope and the one measure

**Community work, not a sale.** A single page with events, how to join and
a contact, on a domain the Chamber controls, before any directory. A
directory only once someone owns its upkeep. Measure: join enquiries; and,
for the studio, introductions to members.

Ask for one thing in return: **a slot at a members' meeting** to talk about
what local businesses actually find hard online. That talk is itself demand
research for the archived long tail.

## Timing

**October**, ahead of any Christmas shopping campaign. Ask when the next
committee meeting and AGM are.

## Who decides

The committee, by consensus, slowly. Find the chair or secretary through
the Gmail address or Facebook page.
