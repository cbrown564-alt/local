## What we know

Sourced from `research/pipeline/verifications.json` and
`research/concepts/newcastle-dental/`.

- 2 Railway Street, Newcastle. Three named dentists; trading under its own
  name. A 2014 origin and an award are recorded in the concept's history.
- **Both of the practice's domains send patients elsewhere.**
  newcastlefamilydentalcare.com redirects over plain HTTP to
  djmaguiredentists.co.uk/location-newcastle.html, a page on a Portadown
  group's site; www doesn't resolve. The .co.uk the practice uses **in its
  own email address** times out on HTTPS and also redirects over plain HTTP
  to the same page.
- The practice's relationship to DJ Maguire Dental Group is **not
  established**: it could be ownership, membership or just hosting.

## The problem we think they have

A patient who types the practice's own address, or the one on its
stationery, lands on another business's page, unencrypted. For a health
practice, that's a privacy and trust issue as well as a lost front door.

## Would they want it?

Everything depends on the ownership question.

- **If independent:** a strong case. The fix is small (their own domain,
  served securely, with their dentists, hours and a registration request),
  and the privacy point is one a practice should want to close.
- **If the group owns it:** the decision isn't in Newcastle, and the redirect
  is probably the group's choice. Archive the dossier.
- **Kill question (answer before visiting):** "Who owns the practice?"

## Pitch to test (only if independent)

> "When someone types your web address, or the one in your email, they end
> up on a different practice's page, and not over a secure connection.
> I've made a version of your own front door that fixes both."

Say this privately and carefully. It's a fault, not an accusation.

## Smallest paid scope and the one measure

**Presence repair:** the practice's own domain under its control, served
over HTTPS, one to three pages (dentists, treatments, hours, NHS / private
position) and a registration or appointment request. Measure: new-patient
registrations through the site; and the insecure redirect gone.

## Timing

Any time, but only once ownership is known.

## Who decides

The owning dentist or the practice manager, if independent; the group if
not.

## Step 0 before the first visit

- **Answer the ownership question first.** Companies House for the
  practice's company, the Business Services Organisation dental list, the
  DJ Maguire group's site and any announcements.
- Re-probe both domains: still redirecting? Still HTTP?

## Unknowns to answer

- Ownership.
- Who registered the domains, and who controls them.
- Whether the redirect is deliberate.
