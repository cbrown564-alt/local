## What we know

- A family-run B&B on Central Promenade, Newcastle, with a purple door.
  Facebook: 829 followers, 23 reviews at 100% recommend; a host named Maria
  appears in a June 2026 review.
- conlynhouse.com (OceanWP / Elementor) already holds what a feed can't:
  **nine named rooms** (The New Yorker, The Regency, Little Havana, Key West
  and five more), **a public rate card of £80–£120**, breakfast 8–10,
  ramp and ground-floor access, parking, checkout at 11:00.
- **"Book Now" is a contact form, and its button reads "Sumbit".** No
  calendar, no deposit terms, no check-in time. The gallery is 39 photos
  from one shoot on 20 May 2019; the copy still says "newly-refurbished".
  The site's Facebook icon has no link.

## The problem we think they have

A guest who has read the rates and chosen a room presses "Book Now" and
gets a form that promises a reply later. The ones who want to book now go
to an OTA and book there, or book somewhere else.

## Would they want it?

- **For:** the site is recent and has real content, so the owners have
  invested before. The failure is at the exact moment of booking. The
  fix is small.
- **Against:** a nine-room B&B may prefer to vet guests by email, or be full
  enough in season not to care. They may take OTA bookings happily.
- **Kill question:** "When someone fills in the Book Now form, what
  happens next, and how many of those become a stay?"

## Pitch to test

> "Your site already shows the rooms and the prices, which most B&Bs don't.
> The one gap is that Book Now doesn't book. I made a version where a guest
> can pick a room and dates and get an answer, using a booking tool you'd
> control."

## Smallest paid scope and the one measure

**Direct action:** a real booking route on the existing site: a specialist
small-accommodation booking engine, or at least an availability request
that captures room and dates. Fix the button label on day one for free.
Measure: direct bookings per month, and the share of nights sold direct
versus OTA.

**Assumption to test, not a fact:** OTA commission for a UK B&B is
commonly around 15%. At £100 a night, each night that moves from an OTA to
direct keeps roughly £15. Ask what they actually pay.

## Timing

**November**, when the season is over and next year's rates and channels
get set.

## Who decides

The owners, likely the couple who run it. Visit mid-morning after
checkout.

## Step 0 before the first visit

- **Capture a real before.** One of the 41 grafts; the case study carries a
  placeholder.
- Check whether "Book Now" is still a form and the button still reads
  "Sumbit".
- Check which OTAs list them, and the rates shown.

## Unknowns to answer

- What happens to form enquiries.
- Who built the site and whether they still maintain it.
