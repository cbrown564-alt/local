## What we know

Sourced from `research/pipeline/verifications.json` and
`research/concepts/donard-veterinary/`.

- 8 Railway Street, Newcastle. Opened 2017; independently owned; small
  animal only. Recruiting in July 2026 (vetni.co.uk), with "no out of
  hours" for staff: out of hours, calls divert to an on-call vet, and
  clients get VidiVet, a free 24/7 digital vet advice service.
- The website is a 2017-era Divi build with a "© 2017" footer, so likely
  unchanged since opening. **Its viewport blocks zooming**, a real
  accessibility fault for older clients on phones. The "Book Appointments"
  page lists a phone number and an email; there's no request form. The
  census recorded a misspelled domain that doesn't resolve.
- The practice's own words are strong, including a bereavement page, "When
  the time comes to say goodbye".

## The problem we think they have

Clients can't ask for a routine appointment outside opening hours except by
email, and the front desk takes every booking by phone. Older clients
can't zoom the site on a phone.

## Would they want it?

- **For:** a growing, independent practice (it's hiring). Routine
  appointments, vaccinations and health plans are recurring revenue, and a
  request form cuts phone time for a busy desk.
- **Against:** many practices use practice-management software with its own
  booking module, and may prefer that to any website form. The phone may be
  deliberate for triage.
- **Kill question:** "How much of the front desk's day is booking routine
  appointments by phone?"

## Pitch to test

> "Your site says exactly the right things, including the out-of-hours
> arrangements. What it can't do is take a routine appointment request at
> 9pm, and it won't let people zoom on a phone. I made a version that does
> both and keeps your words."

## Smallest paid scope and the one measure

**Direct action:** an appointment-request form (species, reason, preferred
day) that reaches the practice inbox, the zoom fault fixed, and the
emergency information one tap from every page. If their practice software
has online booking, link to that instead of building a form. Measure:
routine requests arriving online per month, and front-desk time on booking
calls.

## Timing

No season. Worth approaching any time in October–November. If the recruit
has joined, capacity may be up and more bookings welcome.

## Who decides

The owner, who opened the practice in 2017. Visit at a quiet time; phone
first to ask when that is.

## Step 0 before the first visit

- Re-check the site: still Divi, still blocking zoom, still no form?
- Check whether the practice has joined a group since the July recruitment
  ad. Independent practices are often bought by corporate groups; if this
  one has been, the decision may have moved.
- Find which practice-management software they use, if it's public.

## Unknowns to answer

- Booking volume and how much is phone time.
- Their practice software and whether it offers online booking.
- Whether they're still independent.
