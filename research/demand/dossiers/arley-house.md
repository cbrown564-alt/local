- Rooms are sold through **Booking.com, Airbnb and TripAdvisor**, and by

- arleyhousedundrum.co.uk serves a registrar parking frameset. The last

- Facebook reads as a guestbook, not a brochure: dogs on the lawn,

- The July audit named Arley House one of three immediate prospects: "a
  modest number of incremental or commission-avoided bookings could support

## The problem we think they have

Every stranger who books pays an OTA's commission, because the house has
no place of its own to book. The parked domain also sits on the address
their email uses.

## Would they want it?

This is the most doubtful of the ten, and the reason it stays is to find
out.

- **For:** accommodation is the highest-return lane; OTA commission is a
  number the owners know.
- **Against:** our own brief concluded that "the house never missed [its
  website], because its trade never came from strangers on the web". It
  comes from the phone, word of mouth and returning cyclists. If that's
  true, a booking site solves a problem they don't have.
- **Kill question:** "Roughly how many of your nights come through
  Booking.com or Airbnb, and does the commission bother you?"

If the answer is "hardly any" or "not really", the finding is that
direct-booking economics don't apply to a small word-of-mouth guest house.
Record that; it matters for every B&B in both towns.

## Pitch to test

Two, depending on what they say:

> Commission: "You're paying Booking.com for guests who would happily book
> with you directly if there were a place to do it. One page with your rooms
> and a way to ask for dates is enough."

> Domain: "Your web address shows a parking page and your email runs on
> the same address. That's worth sorting before it lapses, whatever else
> you do."

## Smallest paid scope and the one measure

**Presence repair** first: take control of the domain, a one-page site
with rooms, the Belfast Road door and a phone number, and a Google profile
pointing to it. **Direct action** (an enquiry or booking tool) only if they
want more direct trade. Measure: direct enquiries a month; OTA share over
a season.

## Timing

**November**, after the season, when channel costs for next year get
thought about. The domain point is worth raising at any time; if it
lapses, their email goes with it.

## Who decides

The owners. It's a family house; visit mid-morning.

## Step 0 before the first visit

- **Capture a real before.** One of the 41 grafts.
- Look the domain up in WHOIS: who holds it, and when it expires.
- Check current Booking.com and Airbnb listings: number of rooms, prices.
