## What we know

Sourced from `research/pipeline/verifications.json` and
`research/concepts/hugh-mccanns/`, `research/concepts/avoca-hotel/`.

- 119–121 Central Promenade, Newcastle. A 200-year-old building, three
  generations of one family. Weddings for 40–250 guests, a front bar and
  restaurant, Little Haven guest house (NITB four-star, eight bedrooms
  sleeping seventeen) and a sister property, the **Avoca Hotel** at 93–97
  Central Promenade.
- Hugh McCann's site is maintained and well written, built by Amigo Studios;
  a menu was uploaded in July 2026. **It has no enquiry form, no date
  capture and no availability check.** Every wedding enquiry has to be a
  phone call or an email. Testimonials stop in 2019; the brochure is
  "2021.22".
- The Avoca Hotel's site is a hibu packaged brochure. The July audit rated
  it underperforming: "basic legacy brochure with no prominent
  direct-booking journey". Its own copy: "Elegant & cosy hotel… foot of the
  Mournes… stones throw from the Irish Sea"; King scenic and Family rooms;
  the Wild Hare bar; golf play-and-stay packages.

## The problem we think they have

A couple who find the venue at 11pm on a Sunday, two weeks after getting
engaged, can't ask "is our date free for 120 people?" without waiting to
phone. Some of them email three other venues that *do* have a form, and
book whichever answers first.

For the Avoca: a visitor who wants a sea-view room has no obvious way to
book one on the hotel's own site, so they book it, or somewhere else,
through an OTA.

## Would they want it?

- **For:** the site is maintained, so someone cares about it. Weddings are
  the business, and a wedding is the largest booking either town sells. Two
  properties, one family: one conversation can cover both.
- **Against:** they have an agency (Amigo Studios) and may simply ask them
  to add a form. The phone may be deliberate: venues often want to talk
  before quoting.
- **Kill question:** "When someone emails about a wedding, how quickly does
  it turn into a viewing, and do you lose any on the way?"

## Pitch to test

> "Your site already sells the day beautifully. The one thing it can't do is
> let a couple send you their date and numbers the night they find you. I
> made a version that does, and keeps everything else of yours."

Lead with the enquiry; the Avoca comes up second, as "and the hotel next
door has the same gap for rooms".

Don't pitch replacing the Amigo Studios site. If they have an agency they
like, the honest offer may be the enquiry form alone, or nothing.

## Smallest paid scope and the one measure

- **Hugh McCann's: direct action.** A date-and-guest-count enquiry that
  reaches their inbox with the two facts every venue conversation starts
  with. Measure: qualified wedding enquiries per month, before and after.
- **Avoca: direct action.** A visible booking route on the hotel's own site
  (their engine, or a specialist one). Measure: direct room bookings.

## Timing

**Now to mid-November.** Engagements cluster around Christmas and New Year,
and January is when couples start contacting venues. A form has to be live
before Christmas to catch that; that means a decision by mid-November.
Hibu contracts are subscriptions: ask when the Avoca's renews, because that
date is a natural decision point.

## Who decides

A family business: most likely a family member who handles weddings and
marketing across both properties. Find out who before visiting. The
wedding co-ordinator may be the person who feels the problem; the owner is
the person who pays.

## Step 0 before the first visit

- Re-check both sites. Has Hugh McCann's added an enquiry form since July?
- Check whether Amigo Studios still maintains it (footer, recent changes).
- Check the Avoca's current site, platform and booking route.
- Capture a dated before of the Avoca.

## Unknowns to answer

- Enquiries per month, how they arrive, how many become bookings.
- Whether the phone-first route is a choice.
- The agency relationship, and the hibu contract term.
- Whether one person runs marketing for both properties.
