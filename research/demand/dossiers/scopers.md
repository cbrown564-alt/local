## What we know

Sourced from `research/pipeline/verifications.json`,
`research/concepts/scopers/scopers-pitch.md` and the July audit.

- 169 Main Street, Dundrum. Chef Paul Cunningham (Great British Menu 2021)
  and Jenny. Open four years as of July 2026. Northern Ireland's first
  zero-waste hot food bar; the supper club is the standout offer.
- No website. The chef's old domain (paulcunningham.co) no longer resolves.
  Supper clubs are sold through Facebook and Instagram only. Logged out on a
  phone, Instagram shows no posts and Facebook shows no hours or menu; the
  next supper-club date sits behind a login wall.
- Their Instagram is good: strong food photography, 5,976 followers (July).
  The gap isn't the posting; it's that only followers can reach it.
- The July market audit named Scopers one of three immediate prospects:
  "a business whose story is being told by everyone except the business on
  an owned platform".

## The problem we think they have

The supper club, their highest-value night, can only be found by people who
already follow them and are logged in. Every date sells to the same circle.
A visitor from Good Food Ireland or a tourism page who wants to book can't
see when the next one is.

## Would they want it?

- **For:** events with tickets are a measurable action. Christmas is coming.
  The audit's first-phase list (live menus and hours, supper-club booking,
  vouchers, email) is small.
- **Against:** a busy two-person kitchen may be happy with the circle they
  have. Supper clubs may already sell out, in which case the problem isn't
  demand, it's capacity.
- **Kill question:** "Do your supper clubs sell out? How far ahead?"

## Pitch to test

> "Your Instagram's better than most restaurants in the county. The trouble
> is that someone who isn't following you can't find the next supper club
> date. I made one page that always shows it, and the hours, without anyone
> needing a login."

## Smallest paid scope and the one measure

**Presence repair plus one direct action:** one page with today's hours,
the next supper-club date and a way to buy or reserve, plus a Google
Business Profile that points at it. Christmas vouchers if they want them.
Measure: supper-club seats sold through the page, and voucher sales.

## Timing

**Now to early November.** Christmas supper clubs and gift vouchers sell in
November and December. A page live by mid-November catches that season.

## Who decides

Paul and Jenny. Visit on a quiet weekday afternoon, not service time.
