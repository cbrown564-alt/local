**The mastering step earns its place here.** Spread across the five is 0.6 LU,
against 2.8 LU on the unmastered audition takes of identical input. For a
series meant to be played one after another — or dropped individually beside a
page — that consistency is the difference between a set and five separate
files. Single-pass `loudnorm` lands slightly under the −16 target; the
consistency matters more than the absolute, so this is left alone.

## What to listen for

The open question is **whether this voice fits this job**. Its design brief is
a folklore storyteller — "wise village historian", built to bring Gráinne Ní
Mháille and Fionn mac Cumhaill to life. These scripts are practical: a visitor,
a phone, a snag, a question. That gap is the thing to judge. It could read as
warmth that takes the sting out of criticism, or as a storyteller register
sitting oddly on top of website advice.

Also worth checking: **S5 overlaps the trailer**, since both land the swap
test. If both survive, one has to give.

## Note on the writing

Each script turns on the `decision` field from the page data — the reason the
owner's choice was sensible at the time. *The page was free and the wall came
with it. The video was paid for. Saying it out loud felt like boasting.*

Without that turn these are five accusations in a row, which is both
unlistenable and unfair. It is also the beat most at risk if these are ever cut
for length: it is the part that reads as skippable and is the part doing the
work.

Every check lands as a question, never a verdict — consistent with the page's
"no score, no grade" rule.
