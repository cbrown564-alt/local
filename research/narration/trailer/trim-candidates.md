## Do not cut

- **"Nobody will ever tell you that happened."** The thesis, and the one claim in the piece that is unarguable.
- **"Not one of them is that the site looked ugly."** The line that separates this from every other studio pitch.
- **"Roughly sixty things were wrong… ten patterns."** The only auditable figures in the piece, tracing to `transformation-details.ts`.
- **"That's the work."** / **"We'll show you yours before we ask you for anything."** The turn and the offer.

## If you want to go further than 1:30

The structural argument, not yet taken: **S5 is the swap test in full, 59.5
seconds.** The trailer previews the set — it only has to *plant* the swap test,
not complete it. On that reading the whole of "If nothing else on the page
needs changing… the site isn't yours" is arguably S5's job, and Act 4 becomes
instruction → thesis → offer. That is a bigger edit than a trim and it changes
what the trailer is for, so it is filed as a question rather than a candidate.
