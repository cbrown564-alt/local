# Audition script — long-form narrator

*10 August 2026. Real Long A opening copy, cut to ~450 characters. No place
names, per `research/narrative-audio-plan.md` §3.*

Judged on: does it hold past thirty seconds; does the cadence vary; is it dry
without being cold.

The script is built to expose all three. Three short sentences, then a long
one. A dash of dry understatement in "That's the floor." A paradox to land at
the end, which is where a flat narrator will fall over.

---

Competence is not the same as great.

A good concept gets the information right. The handoffs honest. The colours
coherent. That's the floor.

Great needs two things polish cannot buy. Recognition — details only someone
who studied your business would know. And theatre. One moment of surprise, in
the register you already trade in.

Both of them rest on a third thing. The one claim that is true of you, and
true of no competitor.

It is almost always a paradox.
