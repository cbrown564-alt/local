# Studio storytelling — product showcases & explainers

*Workstream opened 5 August 2026.*

Mourne Made’s offer is easy to undervalue because it is abstract: a right-sized
route from local discovery to booking, order or enquiry, proven first as a free
before-and-after. The capture pairs and the laser focus on web redesign close
part of that gap. They do not yet carry the full weight of *why this studio,
why this method, why care now* — especially for a busy owner who has never
bought digital work.

This workstream exists to build that missing layer: **product showcases,
explainers, and studio-facing stories** — with humour, characters, scenes,
claymation, kinetic type, and generated film/audio as accelerators — without
diluting the honesty of the offer or competing with `/request/`.

It is **not** concept elevation (guest business voice).  
It is **not** sensory Phase 4 (consented film *of* a real business).  
It is studio marketing and teaching, in the studio’s own voice.

## 1. The problem

| What we have | What owners still have to invent |
| --- | --- |
| Side-by-side before/after | What “right-sized” means for *them* |
| Portfolio of concepts | Why an independent concept is not a fake case study |
| About page principles | How recognition + theatre differs from a template makeover |
| Request form | What happens after they submit, in human terms |
| RebuildStage motion | Why the studio’s craft is more than a prettier skin |
| Elevation method in `docs/` | A version a non-designer can feel in ten seconds |

The belief sequence in `PRODUCT.md` is still mostly carried by prose and one
comparison control. Generated media lets us stage that sequence as stories.

## 2. Jobs this workstream must do

Each piece of work should clear at least one job. Name the job in the brief
before generating a clip.

1. **Make the abstract concrete** — show the route (discovery → owned page →
   action) as a scene, not a slogan.
2. **Explain the free before-and-after** — what they send, what they get, what
   they do not owe.
3. **Teach elevation without jargon** — recognition + theatre as felt moments;
   “nicer UI” as the villain.
4. **Separate proof from claim** — independent concepts vs approved clients;
   honesty as a character trait, not a footnote.
5. **Show scopes as choices** — presence repair / direct action / ongoing
   growth as three doors, not a menu of anxiety.
6. **Localise trust** — Dundrum and Newcastle as the room we work in; community
   funding as consequence, not charity theatre.
7. **Earn the request** — every showcase ends closer to `/request/` or
   `/transformations/`, never as spectacle for its own sake.

## 3. Boundaries

### In scope

- Studio pages and prototypes: `/`, `/about/`, `/request/`, `/transformations/`,
  `/prototypes/**`
- Outreach sheets, social cuts, email headers, print companion films
- Internal teaching (elevation method, sensory demos reframed as product story)
- Characters and scenes that are clearly *ours* (studio mascots, clay towns,
  abstract shopfronts) — not likenesses of real owners without consent

### Out of scope (other workstreams own these)

| Topic | Owner |
| --- | --- |
| Guest concept theatre (Kent lights, Scopers plates, etc.) | Concept elevation + `research/film/omni-clip-backlog.md` |
| Per-business establishing film with consent | Sensory Phase 4 |
| Town hero films as homepage atmosphere | `research/film/one-day-made-here.md` |
| Craft process for elevating a concept pack | `docs/the-elevation-method.md` |

### Honesty (non-negotiable)

- No invented clients, testimonials, rankings, or measured results.
- Concepts stay labelled as independent / uncommissioned when they appear in
  studio stories.
- Generated film/audio: provenance + visible disclosure when guest-facing.
- SynthID / “imagined, not footage” is a feature.
- Request path wins on attention weight (`PLAN.md`, sensory plan).

### Voice

Studio marketing **may** narrate the website, the method, and the studio
process. Concept pages **may not** (`AGENTS.md` guest-voice rule). Keep the
two languages in separate files and surfaces.

## 5. Cast & visual grammar (draft — unlock with first slate)

Do not over-design before the first ten clips. Lock only what prevents drift:

- **Place:** County Down coastal light — slate, sea green, warm amber window
  (same family as town films; less tourism-postcard).
- **Studio mark:** mountain-ring / existing wordmark may appear in studio
  pieces; never stamp it onto concept guest pages via this workstream.
- **Character (optional):** one clay or drawn “maker” figure — tools, not cape.
  No fake owner avatars of real locals.
- **Town toys:** small clay Dundrum / Newcastle as stage sets for explainers.
- **Type:** ticket amber / harbour brass for kinetic tests; production prefers
  accessible HTML type over burned-in words when the piece ships on-site.
- **Humour register:** dry, local, specific — never sneering at businesses that
  lack a site.

Do **not** promote a studio explainer into the first viewport until it beats
the current Product Stage belief test: busy owner understands a useful

## 11. Working agreements

1. **Job before gen.** No clip without a job ID (S1–S10 or new).
2. **Sidecar every output.** Prompt, date, model, pass/fail note.
3. **Edit > re-roll.** Same Omni conversational discipline as concept work.
4. **Still under motion.** Reduced-motion always gets a designed poster.
5. **Request path wins.** If a showcase steals weight from `/request/`, cut it.
6. **Concepts are guests.** When a real business appears in a studio story, it
   appears as an independent concept with the same honesty labels as the site.
7. **Humour punches up or sideways** — never at owners for lacking a website.
