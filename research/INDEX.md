# `research/` — what's here

Prose and manifests only. All binary media moved out to `media/` on 11 August
2026 — see [`reorganisation-plan.md`](reorganisation-plan.md) for why, and
[`image-provenance.md`](image-provenance.md) for the index of what each image is.

- **`film/`** — briefs, prompt sheets, theme docs and shot lists for the town
  and studio film work, including the trailer.
- **`narration/`** — scripts, trim decisions and listening notes for the
  ElevenLabs narration passes.
- **`concepts/`** — per-business elevation briefs and image/video prompts.
- **`audits/`** — typography and layout review notes per concept.
- **`media-sprint/`** — job briefs, checkpoints and handoff notes for the
  bounded two-day generation wave opened 9 August 2026.
- **`pipeline/`** — discovery, verification and FSA-matching data behind the
  concept briefs.
- **`prototypes/`** — standalone experiment write-ups not tied to a concept.
- **`image-provenance.md`** — the index of every image: how it was made,
  whether it's in use, and where its file now lives under `media/`.
