## Known faults; do not promote

- T01 needs cleaner boot continuity and a less magical final bridge gesture.
- T04 contains generated clock numerals and needs a longer empty-window hold.
- X18's grey plates do not read clearly enough as before/after, and its lit
  after tile becomes too house-like.
- The reel has no visible generated-media disclosure because it is a research
  file. Any page trial must add the visible “imagined, not footage” disclosure,
  a poster and a reduced-motion state.

## Gate

Internal review answers one question: does fault → fault → offer make the
studio's proposition clearer without making the work look expensive? Corrected
source exports are required before a page trial. This prototype is implemented,
not user-validated or promoted.
