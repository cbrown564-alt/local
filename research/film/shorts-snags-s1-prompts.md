## Fixed visual contract

Miniature clay and paper-craft set, stop-motion animation look, shot slightly
above eye level on a long lens with shallow depth of field. No people, no
faces, no hands, no lettering, no numerals, no logos, no signage, no readable
marks, no interface, no watermark. Calm and dry, not corporate explainer
energy. 16:9.

Keep the same small deep green-painted clay shopfront, dark slate stall-riser,
wet cobbles, blue dusk and warm amber interior as the trailer anchors
`media/film/stills/trailer/03-reimagined.png` and
`media/film/stills/trailer/04-frosted-login-wall.png`. The visitor is only
boots. The page is only physical obstruction: blank paper, frosted glass and
the wall behind the wall.

## v0 prompts

### 03 — double frost

Close continuation of the trailer's green clay shopfront at blue dusk. A
blank pale paper consent slip has fallen flat at the base of the recessed
door. Directly behind the doorway, a second taller sheet of frosted glass has
risen as a wall behind the first frosted pane. The two separate glass planes
must be visible and the amber interior must read dimmer through both. Keep the
wet cobbles and no identifiable frontage. Blank paper only; no interface.

### 04 — boots turn away

The same pair of small clay boots, three-quarters turned away from the original
door, weight shifted off it. Keep the green three-bay shopfront, dark slate
stall-riser, blue dusk, wet cobbles and weak amber light behind the double
frosted barrier. The boots are the only customer sign. Quiet deflation, fair and
non-mocking. No person, shadow, lettering or extra customer object.
