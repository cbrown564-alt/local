# Shorts snags — S2 still pass

*First pass: 11 August 2026. Research only.*

This is the working prompt sheet for S2 — “Nobody answers ‘are you open?’”.
The shot list and timing remain canonical in
[`shorts-snags-visuals-brief.md`](shorts-snags-visuals-brief.md). The eight
frames are a first still pass for the new clock/hours-board object; they are
not approved to ship or to move.

## Fixed visual contract

Miniature clay and paper-craft set, stop-motion animation look, shot slightly
above eye level on a long lens with shallow depth of field. No people, no
faces, no hands, no lettering, no numerals, no logos, no signage, no readable
marks, no interface, no watermark. Calm and dry, not corporate explainer
energy. 16:9.

Keep the established County Down object-theatre world: a small deep
sea-green-painted clay shopfront, dark slate stall-riser, wet cobbles, blue
dusk and warm amber interior. Use the T04 controls
`media/film/stills/studio/T04-01-dark-window.png` and
`T04-02-clock-lights.png` for palette, rain, frontage and clock material. The
visitor is only one closed umbrella in shot 01 and one open umbrella in shot
05. The page is only physical obstruction: brass strip, blank paper poster,
map and low hours plate. No text is required; position and discoverability
carry the idea.

## v0 prompts

### 01 — closed umbrella approaches

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: research still for a narrated short.
Primary request: a single closed clay umbrella moving toward a row of small
generic shopfronts along a wet cobbled street at blue dusk. The umbrella is
the only visitor sign and sits low in the foreground, angled toward one
sea-green shopfront with a dark window and a faint warm interior glow. Leave
the shopfronts generic and unidentifiable. Composition is a calm wide 16:9
approach shot with the umbrella offset slightly from centre and generous street
space ahead of it. Style/medium: miniature clay and paper-craft set,
stop-motion animation look. Lighting/mood: rainy blue dusk, restrained amber
window light, quiet practical anticipation. Palette: slate, sea green, wet
charcoal cobbles, warm amber and small harbour-brass details. Materials:
hand-shaped clay, damp slate, paper-craft frontage, tiny water beads.
Constraints: no people, faces, hands, lettering, numerals, logos, signage,
readable marks, interface or watermark; no identifiable business; no dramatic
weather or corporate explainer energy.

### 02 — strip over the window

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: research still for a narrated short.
Primary request: close continuation of the same generic sea-green clay
shopfront at blue dusk. A thin harbour-brass strip is fixed across the very
top of the fascia, crowded with tiny blank flag-shaped tabs that suggest a
translation bar without containing any marks. Beneath it, a blank paper poster
fills most of the dark shop window and emits a soft looping flicker of warm
light, suggesting a video without depicting an image or screen. Keep the
window and frontage physical, not a website interface. Composition is a
slightly-above-eye-level 16:9 close shot, with the strip near the top edge and
the poster below. Style/medium: miniature clay and paper-craft set,
stop-motion animation look. Lighting/mood: blue rain-dark exterior, weak warm
flicker behind glass, calm and matter-of-fact. Palette and materials must match
`T04-01-dark-window.png` and `T04-02-clock-lights.png`. Constraints: no people,
faces, hands, lettering, numerals, logos, signage, readable marks, interface or
watermark; all paper and tabs blank; no identifiable business.
