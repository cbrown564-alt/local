# Shorts snags — S4 still pass

*First pass: 11 August 2026. Research only.*

This is the working prompt sheet for S4 — “The best thing about you is in the
footer”. The shot list and timing remain canonical in
[`shorts-snags-visuals-brief.md`](shorts-snags-visuals-brief.md). The first
complete still pass is research-only and is not approved to ship or to move.

## Fixed visual contract

Miniature clay and paper-craft set, stop-motion animation look, shot slightly
above eye level on a long lens with shallow depth of field. No people, no
faces, no hands, no lettering, no numerals, no logos, no signage, no readable
marks, no interface, no watermark. Calm and dry, not corporate explainer
energy. 16:9.

Keep the established County Down object-theatre world: slate roofs, deep
sea-green painted clay frontage, blue dusk and restrained warm amber window
light. The three businesses are intentionally generic and nearly identical.
The visitor is only a folded paper note in later shots. The page is physical
paper and brass, never a literal screen or readable copy.

## v0 prompts

### 01 — three indistinguishable shopfronts

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: research still for a narrated short.
Primary request: three small clay shopfronts standing side by side on a quiet
cobbled street at blue dusk, all three with the same slate roof, deep
sea-green painted frontage, plain rendered walls and dark windows, so they are
deliberately indistinguishable from this distance. No signs or names. A faint
restrained warm amber glow sits behind each window. Calm wide 16:9 composition,
slightly above eye level, long lens and shallow depth of field; leave a little
space between the shopfronts for a visitor to later pause.
Style/medium: miniature clay and paper-craft set, stop-motion animation look.
Lighting/mood: blue coastal dusk, quiet uncertainty, no drama.
Color palette: slate, sea green, wet charcoal cobbles, warm amber and small
harbour-brass details.
Materials: hand-shaped clay, paper-craft frontage, damp cobbles, softly
imperfect painted surfaces.
Constraints: no people, faces, hands, lettering, numerals, logos, signage,
readable marks, interface or watermark; no identifiable business.

### 04 — low brass plaque

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: research still for a narrated short.
Primary request: ground-level close-up of a small tarnished harbour-brass
plaque fixed to the base of a generic deep sea-green clay shopfront, sitting
beside the dark slate stall-riser on the cobbles. The plaque is low enough to
be easy to walk past and catches almost no light; its face is blank and
unreadable, but it should clearly feel like a modest record, award or
accreditation plate. Keep the rest of the shopfront soft and high in frame,
with the plaque small in the lower third rather than filling the image. Static
16:9 act-break composition, slightly above eye level relative to the plaque,
long lens and shallow depth of field.
Style/medium: miniature clay and paper-craft set, stop-motion animation look.
Lighting/mood: blue dusk with one weak warm reflection, modest and overlooked,
not punitive or mocking.
Color palette: slate, sea green, tarnished brass, blue-grey shadow and a small
amount of warm amber.
Materials: hand-shaped clay, worn brass, rough cobbles, paper-craft frontage.
Constraints: no people, faces, hands, lettering, numerals, logos, signage,
readable marks, interface or watermark; the plaque must remain blank.
