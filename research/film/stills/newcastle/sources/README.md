# Newcastle still grounding sources

These photographs are retained as internal geographic references for the six
generated Newcastle film stills. The stills transform atmosphere and sparse
activity while preserving the documented places and camera relationships.

All Geograph photographs listed below are licensed under Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 2.0 and were downloaded 5 August 2026.

## Generated derivatives

The six sibling PNGs one directory above were created with the built-in OpenAI
image generation tool. Stills 02 and 04 are atmosphere edits of 01, preserving
one beach master. Stills 03, 05 and 06 each use their own documented camera
position because Central Promenade, the tidal harbour and Kent Amusements are
separate real places. Tool outputs were cropped, without adding content, and
normalised to 1600×900. They are generated visualisations, not documentary
photographs or evidence of exact weather, people, business activity or light.
