shot list and timing remain canonical in
[`shorts-snags-visuals-brief.md`](shorts-snags-visuals-brief.md). The first

## Fixed visual contract

Miniature clay and paper-craft set, stop-motion animation look, shot slightly
above eye level on a long lens with shallow depth of field. No people, no
faces, no hands, no lettering, no numerals, no logos, no signage, no readable
marks, no interface, no watermark. Calm and dry, not corporate explainer
energy. 16:9.

Keep the same deep green-painted clay frontage, dark slate counter, blue dusk
and warm amber interior as the trailer bell anchor
`media/film/stills/trailer/08-silent-bell.png`. The bell is the recurring
object: small, worn brass, on a dark slate or wood ledge. The visitor is only
the folded paper note or the implied boot-toe; never show a person.

## v1 prompt

Close continuation of the trailer's small worn brass counter bell, reframed
as a compact wall-mounted door bell beside a recessed clay shopfront door at
blue dusk. Keep the bell small and rounded, with the same worn brass and dark
base as the trailer anchor; it must not become a large intercom or oversized
door fitting. A tiny plain boot-toe enters only at the bottom edge and is just
touching the bell button; no leg, person, face or hand. Keep the dark slate
threshold, deep green painted clay, shallow warm amber interior and quiet
object-theatre mood from the trailer anchor. Blank surfaces only; no lettering,
numbers, logos, signage or interface.
