# Studio media experiments — prompt-ready sidecars

*Written 7 August 2026. Standalone bakeoff slate for generated media that
explains what the studio does. Companion to
[`studio-storytelling.md`](../studio-storytelling.md) (jobs, honesty, phases)
and [`studio-recurring-themes.md`](studio-recurring-themes.md) (T1–T10 clay
scenes — the control). This file does not replace those; it widens the format
bakeoff on purpose.*

**Tools.** GPT-Image 2 (stills / plates / boards), Gemini Omni (≈6–10s video),
ElevenLabs (VO + Foley beds). Most clips will fail. That is the point.

**Outputs.** Save under `media/film/clips/studio/` as `X<id>-<slug>.{png,mp4,webm,mp3}`
with a filled sidecar copy of the relevant section below (date, model, pass/fail).
Contact-sheet before promoting anything to `/prototypes/`.

---

## House rules (every gen)

1. **Job before gen.** Every sidecar names a job (S1–S10, T1–T10, or offer gap).
2. **No faces, no hands, no lettering in-frame** unless the sidecar explicitly
   opts into burned-in social type (X13 only). Blank fascias stay blank.
3. **Object theatre default.** Customer = boots, umbrella, bicycle, folded note.
   The *page* is the antagonist, never the owner.
4. **One physical turn** when staging a fault: fault felt → fix as one gesture.
   Offer/method pieces may use a different beat structure — named in the sidecar.
5. **Still under motion.** Beat-two (or final) frame is the poster; if the still
   does not carry the idea, the scene failed.
6. **SynthID / “imagined, not footage”** is a feature. Provenance entry before
   commit; visible disclosure if guest-facing.
7. **No invented clients, testimonials, rankings, or measured results.**
8. **Request path wins.** Spectacle that steals weight from `/request/` dies.
9. **Edit > re-roll.** Half the day’s gens reserved for conversational edits.
10. **Kill criterion is belief, not beauty.** *Would a busy owner understand a
    useful commercial change in ten seconds?* Pretty-but-mute dies.

**Palette.** County Down coastal: slate, sea green, warm amber window, harbour
brass. Dry, specific humour — never sneering at businesses that lack a site.

**Acceptance line (default).** A viewer with no sound and no surrounding copy
can say what went wrong or what was offered. Sidecars may tighten this.

### X1 — Wet-plate shopfront

**Job.** T4 (open?) with T1 flavour (barrier at the door).  
**Stack.** GPT-Image 2 still → Omni I2V → optional ElevenLabs soft Foley.  
**Message.** People arrive with one question. The first screen should answer it.

**Still brief (GPT-Image 2).**

```
Square or 16:9. Wet-plate collodion / tintype photograph of a tiny coastal
shopfront at dusk, as if shot in the 1890s: irregular silvering, soft chemical
fog at the edges, warm amber window fighting through the fog, wet slate
pavement, no people, no faces, no hands, blank fascia, no lettering, no logos.
County Down coastal mood. The window is almost unreadable — fogged plate.
Museum-scan quality, not Instagram filter.
```

**Scene.** The plate’s chemical fog is the unread window. An umbrella-shaped
shadow approaches in the emulsion, hesitates, withdraws. Then the fog lifts
only in a brass-rimmed circle (a clock face with hands, no numerals); amber
reaches the pavement; the shadow returns.

**Storyboard.**

| Beat | s | Frame |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0.0–2.0 | Static tintype; fogged window; rain suggestion in silvering. |
| 2 | 2.0–4.0 | Dark umbrella silhouette approaches glass; tilts; nothing clears. |
| 3 | 4.0–5.5 | Silhouette withdraws. Empty fogged plate. **(loss)** |
| 4 | 5.5–7.0 | Fog clears in a brass circle; amber blooms on pavement. |
| 5 | 7.0–8.0 | Silhouette returns; folds at door. Freeze for loop / poster. |

**Omni prompt.**

```
Image-to-video from the attached wet-plate still, 8 seconds. Keep the tintype /
collodion look for the entire clip — irregular silvering, soft chemical fog,
no modern colour grade. No people, no faces, no hands. A dark umbrella
silhouette approaches the fogged shop window, tilts as if trying to read, then
withdraws out of frame. Hold on the empty fogged plate. Then the chemical fog
clears only inside a plain brass-rimmed circle in the window (clock hands, no
numerals); warm amber light spreads onto the wet pavement. The umbrella
silhouette returns and settles at the door.

No text, no lettering, no logos, no signage. Blank fascia stays blank.
Motion: slow, photographic, almost still — like a plate developing, not a
commercial.
```

**Audio (optional).** Soft rain + one glass-clear chime on the fog lift. No VO.

**Edit turns.** 1. `Preserve tintype grain and fog — do not modernise colour.`
2. `No lettering anywhere.` 3. `Hold empty fogged plate longer.` 4. `Freeze last
half-second.`

**Acceptance.** Works as a postcard front at beat 3. Kill if it just looks
“vintage pretty” without the unread → read turn.

**Job.** S6 — nicer is not elevation — without staging template sludge as the

```

text, no UI chrome, no faces, no hands. It should feel like an empty product
```

**Acceptance.** Feels like a genre trap that snaps shut. Kill if the first five
