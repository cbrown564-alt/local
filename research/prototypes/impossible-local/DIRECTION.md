# Impossible local website — direction decision

Recorded 9 August 2026 before completing the representative prototype.

## Physical scene

A mechanic closes the workshop after rain. Under one hard inspection lamp, a
strip of silver reflective tape carries the ridge line across a black bench;
one cobalt anodised part is the only saturated colour.

Voice words: **cut, reflective, loaded**.

## Compared directions

### Reflective instrument — selected

The route is a bright physical line. Height readings, chapter positions and a
cobalt inspection point make it legible at street level; at high ground the
same samples separate into reflective fins and distant layers. It uses no
photography and avoids pretending a generated landscape is geographic proof.

### Wet mineral plane — rejected

The held high-ground image study established a convincing settled frame, but
using it as the scene would make generated atmosphere do work that belongs to
the terrain data. It remains a research reference only.

### Safety-signal workshop — rejected

Signal orange, ticket typography and exposed mechanical controls made the
workshop clear but pushed the route toward generic cycle-campaign language. It
also sat too close to the existing Mourne Cycles industrial treatment.

## Consequence

The selected prototype uses one near-black workbench field, reflective silver
and cobalt inspection light. Canvas, SVG and live readouts all consume the
same route profile. No theme system survives the comparison and no generated
visual is promoted to `public/`.
