Original priority replacements · implemented

These homepage (or featured) images are the clearest cases of over-indexing on documentary truth at the expense of presence. Each should leave the hero slot for mood, conceptual illustration, or a functional element.

Replace

Donard Veterinary — Railway Street exterior

Flat overcast street record. Half the frame is Post Office red, parked cars, and a cropped Skoda in the foreground. The clinic itself is a minor strip of teal signage. Truthful about the address; useless as a care brand signal.

Prefer: warm clinic mood (hands/animal care illustration), calm pet atmosphere, or keep the functional services rail and drop the photo panel.

Replace

Kent Amusements — promenade exterior

Grey day, gold hatchback dominant, road signs and bins competing with the arcade. The brand promise is “fifty summers of seaside fun”; the photo sells traffic and weather. Neon inside is visible but lost.

Prefer: night/neon arcade mood, dodgems/VR detail, illustrated seaside mark, or a functional “today’s attractions / hours” panel (Scopers pattern).

Replace

Mourne Cycles — bike + bin visualisation

Faithful to a customer photo, and honestly captioned — but the hero is a charcoal Trek against a screaming orange fence and blue bin “48”. Reads as alley snapshot, not workshop expertise or Mournes riding.

Prefer: trail/mood riding imagery, clean product cutout, workshop tool illustration, or a functional “book a service / Cycle to Work” desk panel.

Replace

Castle Farm — delivery crate

Literal packed crate under harsh light: plastic glare, supermarket tomato brand label, raw trays. Honest about what arrives; visually unappetising as the first brand impression for a farm produce story.

Prefer: styled produce still-life, field/soil mood, illustrated box mark, or a functional “this week’s box / order” panel.

Replace / demote

Enniskeen — Room 9 bathroom (featured)

Beige-on-beige inventory photo energy. Useful as a truthful room detail deep in a gallery; weak as a featured room visual next to the pink Room 6 character piece and the strong balcony mood shot.

Prefer: keep for disclosure/detail only, or swap featured slot for room atmosphere (bedding, view, seating) rather than sink inventory.

Working principle

Use this test before any concept homepage image ships:

Question If no…
Does the image make the brand’s promise feel true in under one second? Don’t use it in the hero — even if it is geographically accurate.
Is the subject the business experience (care, food, ride, stay, fun) — not parked cars / neighbour signage / bins? Crop harder or abandon the plate.
Would removing the photo and putting a booking/menu/hire panel there be clearer? Prefer the functional panel (Scopers / Tool Centre).
If using AI or mood imagery, is the disclosure honest and the atmosphere on-brand? Enniskeen / Buck’s Head pattern — keep; Mourne Cycles alley — don’t.
